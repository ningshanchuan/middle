# 天才课堂的核心思想 · Core ideas of Genius Classroom

## 中文

**一、美育是核心。** 帮助学生在学习中发现美：
- 知识之美、自然之美、人之美、社会之美；
- 生命之美、宇宙之美、道德之美、历史之美；
- 既有秀美，也有壮美。

**二、斯美也，有丑也。** 面向中学生，以美为主；在合适的地方，对人类历史作少量、适度、实事求是的反思，例如战争、奴役、殖民、屠杀和对自然的破坏。克制，不渲染。

**三、人类命运共同体。** 各国人民相互依存，共同面对挑战。我们展现文明之间的交流互鉴与合作。

**四、人民史观。** 人民群众是历史的创造者。讲历史时，既讲帝王将相，更讲农民、工人、工匠、科学家、移民、士兵、妇女和儿童的劳动、创造与生活。

**五、实事求是。** 每一个事实、日期、数字、引文和出处都要能够核实：
- 不夸大，不回避；
- 不确定或有争议的地方，如实说明。

**怎样落实：**
- 每一课都有一个自然的"美在哪里"的时刻。
- 历史课写到普通人，并在合适时作简短的反思。
- 课标内的内容，充实；课标以外的内容，标明"课外拓展"。
- 每一课都经过独立校对和人工验收，然后才发布。

## English

1. **Aesthetic education (美育) is the core.** We help students discover beauty in everything they learn:
   - the beauty of knowledge, nature, people and society;
   - the beauty of life, the universe, moral courage and history;
   - both graceful beauty and the sublime.
2. **Where there is beauty, there is also ugliness.** For middle schoolers the main tone is beauty. Where it fits, we add a small, age-appropriate and factual reflection on the darker parts of human history, such as war, slavery, colonialism, genocide and harm to nature. It is restrained and never sensational.
3. **A shared future for humankind.** Peoples depend on one another and meet common challenges together. We show exchange, mutual learning and cooperation between civilizations.
4. **History is made by the people.** Besides rulers and generals, we show farmers, workers, craftspeople, scientists, migrants, soldiers, women and children: their work, inventions and lives.
5. **Seek truth from facts.** Every fact, date, number, quotation and attribution must be verifiable and sourced. We do not exaggerate or leave out inconvenient facts, and we say so when something is uncertain or disputed.

**In practice:**
- Every lesson has a natural "where is the beauty here?" moment.
- History lessons include ordinary people, and a short reflection where it fits.
- Topics in the standards are enriched; topics beyond them are marked *Enrichment · 课外拓展*.
- Every lesson is independently reviewed and checked by hand before it is published.
