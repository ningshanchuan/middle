Reading narratives: one back, four times

Zhu Ziqing’s famous essay 《背影》 (“The Sight of Father’s Back”, 1925) is only about 1,500 characters long — yet the word 背影 (bèiyǐng, “the view of someone’s back”) appears four times. Why write about his father’s back, not his face? Reading a narrative (记叙文 jìxùwén) means spotting the choices the writer made.

Your mission: become a story detective — tag the six elements, follow the clue thread, switch the narrator and the order of events, label description methods, and finally build full-mark answers with 中考 (zhōngkǎo, the high-school entrance exam) answer templates.

Mission 1: the six-element detective

Every narrative answers six questions: when (时间), where (地点), who (人物), and the event’s cause (起因), development (经过) and result (结果) — like “setting, characters, plot” in US ELA. Pick a label, then tap the matching words. Finish both stories (written for this site) and you can summarise any story.

The six elements

六要素 liù yàosù

  • 时间 / 地点 — when and where it happens.
  • 人物 — who is in it.
  • 起因 → 经过 → 结果 — why it starts, how it unfolds, how it ends. 经过 is usually the longest part.
🎵 Memory hook“When, where, who — why, how, and then?” Ask the six questions and the story is clear.
⚠️ Don’t mix up:A summary is not copying sentences! Formula: who + in what situation + did what + with what result, usually under 40 characters.

Mission 3: label the description methods

Character description comes in five kinds — appearance (外貌), action (动作), speech (语言), thoughts (心理) and expression (神态) — plus setting description (环境). The texts are real: Zhu Ziqing’s 《背影》 (1925) and Lu Xun’s 《从百草园到三味书屋》 (“From Hundred-Plant Garden to Three-Flavour Study”, 1926), both public domain; the third is our own.

Why the back?

Is a back stronger than a face? ▸

From the train the son can only see his father’s back as he climbs the platform. A clumsy, struggling back shows deep love without a single sentimental word.

What’s so good about 攀, 缩, 倾? ▸

Three verbs — climb (pān), pull up (suō), lean (qīng) — show a heavy, older man struggling. The harder it is, the more we feel his love.

How many times does the writer cry? ▸

Four: at the family’s sad news, watching his father buy oranges, when the back disappears in the crowd, and reading his father’s letter. The tears form a second, emotional thread.

Mission 4: build the full-mark 中考 answer

Many 中考 reading questions follow a pattern: name the method, say what it describes, then give the effect or feeling. Put the three right pieces into the boxes in order — careful, some pieces only look right.

📐 Effect of a description ▸

“运用了__描写，生动形象地写出了__，表现了人物__的性格/心情，表达了作者__的情感。”

📐 Role of the clue / title ▸

“以__为线索，贯穿全文，使结构紧凑；集中体现了……，寄托了作者……的情感；（标题）设置悬念，吸引读者。”

📐 Effect of order / narrator ▸

Flashback: creates suspense, hooks the reader. Insertion: fills in background, enriches the character. 1st person: feels real and personal, easy to express feelings. 3rd person: free across time and place, more objective.
