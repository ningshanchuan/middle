Rhetorical devices: make a sentence come alive

“The waterfall is high.” Boring. “Flying, it plunges straight down three thousand feet!” Now you can see it. Li Bai never measured the waterfall — he used hyperbole. Almost every Chinese exam asks: “Which rhetorical device does this sentence use, and what is its effect?”

Your mission: meet the 8 devices Chinese exams love, become a rhetoric detective, upgrade plain sentences, and learn the exam formula for explaining what a device does.

Mission 1: meet the eight devices

Tap each card. The panel “X-rays” a real example: coloured labels show the parts of the sentence, plus how to spot it, the exam template and the classic mix-up. English names help you link them to what you learn in ELA.

Tip: simile, personification and hyperbole make pictures vivid; parallelism and antithesis give rhythm and force; hypophora, rhetorical questions and quotations make readers think, add emphasis or persuade.

Three look-alike pairs

PairHow to tell them apart
比喻／拟人Is there a picture B (像 something)? Simile. Does the thing just act like a person? Personification.
设问／反问Hypophora answers itself right after; a rhetorical question gives no answer — it’s hidden inside.
排比／对偶Parallelism: 3+ parts, lengths may differ. Antithesis: exactly 2 lines of equal length.

Mission 2: X-ray a whole text — 《陋室铭》

《陋室铭》 (“An Inscription on My Humble Room”, Liu Yuxi, Tang dynasty) has only 81 characters but packs in several devices. Find the line the prompt asks for; after four finds you unlock the lenses to see them all.

⚠️ Don’t mix up:One sentence can use several devices at once: 苔痕上阶绿，草色入帘青 is antithesis AND personification (the moss “climbs”, the grass “comes in”). 孔子云：何陋之有？ is a quotation AND a rhetorical question. In an exam, answer the device the question asks about.

Mission 5: the exam formula — “what does this device do?”

A full-marks answer usually has three steps: ① the device (“uses …”), ② what it shows (“vividly shows the feature of …”), ③ the feeling or effect (“expresses …”). Study the templates, then build four answers yourself.

Textbook links (统编版): 春 and 济南的冬天 (Grade 7 vol. 1, unit 1), 从百草园到三味书屋 (Grade 7 vol. 1, unit 3), 木兰诗 (Grade 7 vol. 2, unit 2), 陋室铭 and 爱莲说 (Grade 7 vol. 2, unit 4), 岳阳楼记 and 醉翁亭记 (Grade 9 vol. 1, unit 3).
