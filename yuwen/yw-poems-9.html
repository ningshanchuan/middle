      Advanced · Compare two poems about the moon

      1. Quote a detail. Du Fu calls the moon brighter back home. Su Shi wishes that people far apart could share the same moon.
      2. Notice a word. “Brighter” reveals how Du Fu feels about home. “Share” shows how the moon can connect Su Shi to someone far away.
      3. Explain the difference. Du Fu’s moon brings out his longing for family scattered by war. Su Shi’s moon lets him turn longing into a wish for his brother’s well-being.
      Challenge · How do the endings of the two Shanpoyang songs compare?

      Both endings repeat the same outcome across opposite cases. At Tong Pass, ordinary people suffer whether a dynasty rises or falls. At Mount Li, rulers who win and rulers who lose both eventually turn to dust.
