Reading expository texts: explain a bridge so anyone gets it

The Zhaozhou Bridge has stood for about 1,400 years, with a main arch spanning 37.02 m (121 ft). But how long is that, really? A good expository text (说明文 shuōmíngwén — like a US “informational text”) uses numbers, comparisons and images so you can see it at once.

Your mission: hunt down eight explaining methods (说明方法) in three science texts, put explanations in the right order, run the “delete-a-word” experiment, and win the Method Blitz by naming both the method and its effect.

Mission 1: the method sorter

Three short science texts written for this lesson (facts from public sources). Pick a method, then tap the sentence that uses it. Some sentences use two methods — either counts.

Three questions

Ask three things first

  • 说明对象 (object) — what is explained? (the bridge, the gardens, the telescope)
  • 说明特征 (features) — what is special about it? (old, cleverly designed, huge…)
  • 说明方法 (methods) — which tools make it clear?
🎵 Memory hook“Define, example, number, compare — picture it, group it, quote it, draw it in words.”
⚠️ Don’t mix up:In an expository text, “像……” is called 打比方 (a method of explaining), not 比喻 (a figure of speech). Write 运用了打比方的说明方法.

Two kinds of expository text

Explaining a thing ▸

Describes a real object’s shape, structure and features — e.g. a bridge, a garden, a telescope.

Explaining a “why” ▸

Explains a process or a cause: why the sky is blue, why dinosaurs died out.

Define vs. explain? ▸

A definition (下定义) captures the essence and can be reversed; an explanation (作诠释) only explains one aspect. Exams love this pair.

Mission 2: put it in order

Three kinds of order: time (时间顺序), space (空间顺序) and logic (逻辑顺序). Tap the sentences in the right order, then say which kind it is.

Mission 3: the delete-a-word experiment

A classic 中考 question: “Can the highlighted word be deleted? Why?” Tap the word to strike it out, read what the sentence now claims, then choose what went wrong.

Mission 4: the Method Blitz

Two steps per sentence: ① name the method, ② choose its effect. Both right = full points, streaks give a bonus. Then you see the model 中考 answer.

📐 The 中考 answer template

运用了××的说明方法，（具体）说明了（对象）的（特征），使说明更加准确具体 / 生动形象 / 条理清楚 / 有说服力。 — “It uses method to explain object’s feature, making the explanation more precise / vivid / organised / convincing.”
