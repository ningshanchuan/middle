Advanced close reading

Start by finding the verb and what it acts on. In 何陋之有, 有 means “to have,” and 何陋 is its object. 之 marks the object placed before the verb. Put the words back in the order 有何陋 to read the question as “What is humble about it?”

Try it. What does 之 do in 友人惭，下车引之?

引 means “to pull” or “to take hold of.” Here 之 means “him,” referring to Yuanfang. The ashamed visitor gets down from his carriage and reaches for Yuanfang.

Compare 舍 in 太丘舍去 and 其人舍然大喜. How do you read each one?

In 舍去, read shě, meaning “give up.” Chen gives up waiting and leaves. In 舍然, read shì, with the meaning of 释. The worried man’s fears lift.
