Chinese Language Arts · Reading · Argumentative essays (议论文)

Reading arguments: how do you convince someone who disagrees?

Tu Youyou’s team screened over 2,000 herbal recipes and failed again and again before sample No. 191 finally worked — then she won the 2015 Nobel Prize. If you want to prove “persistence pays off”, that story beats a hundred repetitions of “keep going!” That is the power of evidence (论据 lùnjù).

Your mission: take an argumentative essay (议论文 yìlùnwén) apart — find the thesis and the evidence, judge what counts as a thesis, spot four methods of argument, then build your own argument and push the persuasion meter to full.

Mission 1: take an essay apart

An argumentative essay has three parts: the thesis (论点 — my claim), the evidence (论据 — the material that proves it) and the reasoning (论证 — how the evidence proves the claim). Like “claim, evidence, reasoning” in US ELA. The model essay below is written for this site — pick a label, then tap a sentence.

Three parts

论点 · 论据 · 论证

  • 中心论点 — the one main claim. 分论点 (sub-claims) support it from different angles.
  • 事实论据 (factual evidence) — events, history, data. 道理论据 (reasoning evidence) — quotations, proverbs, scientific laws.
  • The thesis usually sits in the title, first or last paragraph, as one complete statement.
🎵 Memory hook“The thesis is the target, evidence is the arrow, reasoning is how you shoot.”
⚠️ Don’t mix up:A famous quote is not automatically evidence — it depends on its job. If it proves the claim, it’s 论据; if it states the author’s own view, it’s the 论点.

Mission 2: spot the four methods of argument

Example (举例论证 — show facts), reasoning (道理论证 — cite principles), contrast (对比论证 — set right against wrong), analogy (比喻论证 — explain by a picture). This second essay is ours too; it quotes one line from Francis Bacon’s “Of Studies” (1625).

📐 What each method does (中考 template)

  • 举例论证: “通过列举……的事例，具体有力地证明了……的观点” — concrete and convincing.
  • 道理论证: “引用……，使论证更有权威性和说服力” — adds authority.
  • 对比论证: “将……和……对比，鲜明突出地证明了……” — makes the point stand out.
  • 比喻论证: “把……比作……，生动形象、通俗易懂地证明了……” — vivid and easy.

Compare with English writing

What is CER? ▸

US classrooms use Claim–Evidence–Reasoning — exactly 论点–论据–论证.

What about counter-arguments? ▸

Strong essays admit the other side’s point, then show its limits (“诚然……但是…” — “Admittedly… but…”). English calls this a counterclaim and rebuttal.

What makes good evidence? ▸

True (no made-up stories about famous people), typical, and directly on point.

Mission 3: the argument builder

Pick a thesis. From the evidence cards choose the three that really prove it, and name the method each one uses. Good picks raise the persuasion meter; bad ones (off-topic, against you, or not evidence at all) knock it down. Three good cards and your mini-essay is built!

Mission 4: build the full-mark answer

The favourite exam question: “Which method does this paragraph use, and what is its effect?” Put the right pieces in order: method → content → effect.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Standard: 《义务教育语文课程标准（2022年版）》 stage 4: read simple argumentative essays, tell claims from material (principles, facts, data, charts), see how they connect, and judge for yourself; task group “critical reading and expression”.

Textbook (统编版): Grade 9 vol. 1, units 2 and 5 are the argumentative units (e.g. 《敬业与乐业》, 《论教养》, 《谈创造性思维》); copyrighted textbook essays are not reproduced — the model essays are ours. US link: Georgia K-12 ELA Standards 6–8 — argument writing and evaluating claims, evidence and reasoning, incl. counterclaims.

Sources: Tu Youyou’s team screened 2,000+ herbal preparations, found 640 hits and tested 380+ extracts (Tu, Nature Medicine, 2011); “sample No. 191” (the 1971 neutral ether extract) is the standard Chinese account. Sima Guang’s 《资治通鉴》 took 19 years; Shang Yang’s “moving the log” is in 《史记》; other quotes from 《论语》, 《礼记·大学》, 《孟子》, 《荀子·劝学》. The Bacon line is a common Chinese rendering of “Some books are to be tasted, others to be swallowed, and some few to be chewed and digested.”
