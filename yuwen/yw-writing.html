Writing in Chinese: make the reader see it

“妈妈很累。” (Mom is tired.) — five characters, and the reader sees nothing.
“她把最后一个碗放进柜子，一只手扶着腰，慢慢直起身。” (She puts the last bowl in the cupboard, one hand on her lower back, and slowly straightens up.) — now we see it. In ELA this is called “show, don’t tell.” In the 中考 the essay is usually worth around 50 points, a third or more of the whole Chinese exam — and the high marks hide in details like these.

Your mission: find the key words (题眼 tíyǎn, “eye of the title”) in exam prompts and pick the best angle, upgrade flat sentences with details, put a scrambled model essay back in order and read its margin notes, then master opening and ending techniques. Complete missions to rise from 书童 (page boy) to 状元 (top scholar)!

What is a 题眼? ▸

The words that limit what you may write and where the focus goes. In “That moment, I grew up”, 那一刻 demands ONE moment, 长大了 demands a change.

Three tests for a good angle ▸

Accurate (hits the key words), deep (from an event to an insight), fresh (small, true material — not what everyone writes).

Filling a blank in the title ▸

Go small and concrete: “a pot of pothos plant” beats “human progress” every time.

⚠️ Don’t mix up:Flashback-first (倒叙 dàoxù) doesn’t mean writing the whole essay backwards: you show the ending or the most memorable moment first, then go back and tell it in order. An inserted flashback (插叙 chāxù) is a memory dropped into the middle — afterwards you return to the main line.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Standard: 《义务教育语文课程标准（2022年版）》, task group “literary reading and creative expression”; stage-4 writing: observe life from many angles and capture the features of things; write narratives with a clear purpose and concrete, full content; write at least 500 characters in 45 minutes.

Textbook links (统编版): Grade 7 vol 1 writing units “learning to narrate events” and “keeping a clear central idea”; Grade 7 vol 2 “capturing details” and “choosing material”; Grade 8 vol 1 “describing scenery” (check your edition). Prompt analysis and openings/endings are standard 中考 essay revision.

Notes: the model essay, all upgrader sentences and the angle options are original. Famous openings/endings are short quotations (Lu Xun and Zhu Ziqing are public domain). 中考 essay weight varies by province (usually 50 or 60 points out of 120 or 150), so “around 50” is approximate. The “Now you try” helper uses simple keyword checks and can’t replace a teacher. US link: the Georgia K-12 ELA standards (grades 6–8) ask narrative writers for descriptive details, sensory language and a reflective conclusion — the same “show, don’t tell”.
