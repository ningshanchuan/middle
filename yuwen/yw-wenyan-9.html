Word check: Does 景 mean the same thing twice?

In 春和景明 from the Tower essay, 明 (bright) points to sunlight. In 四时之景不同 from the Pavilion essay, the flowers, shade, frost and rocks point to scenery. Use nearby words to test what a familiar character means.

How does the pavilion essay move from the governor’s enjoyment of the landscape to his enjoyment of other people’s happiness? Use two details.

Show model answer

“醉翁之意不在酒，在乎山水之间也” locates the governor’s first pleasure in the scenery. In “太守之乐其乐” he takes pleasure in what the visitors enjoy. The ending lets his own pleasure grow into joy shared with the people.
