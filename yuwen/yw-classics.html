Great Books: the 12 set novels, memoirs and poems of Chinese middle school

A monkey who picks a fight with Heaven. A rickshaw puller in old Beijing who just wants his own rickshaw. A submarine cruising under the sea — in a book from 1870. An English orphan girl who says “I am no bird.” Every Chinese middle-schooler reads these 12 books in grades 7–9, and the 中考 (zhōngkǎo, the high-school entrance exam) always asks about them.

Your mission: browse the great-books shelf, walk the road to the West with the monk and his three disciples, untangle the web of the Liangshan outlaws, then guess “which book am I?” from three clues. Complete missions to rise from 书童 (page boy) to 状元 (top scholar)!

Six ways to read a big book

Close reading & skipping (精读和跳读) ▸

Read the key chapters slowly, twice; skim repeated fight scenes and verses. That’s how to tackle the 100 chapters of Journey to the West.

Marking & margin notes (圈点批注) ▸

Circle striking words, then write your reactions, questions and judgements in the margin — like annotating in ELA class.

Speed reading (快速阅读) ▸

Take in a whole line at a glance, don’t go back, chase the plot first.

Copying & note-taking (摘抄笔记) ▸

Copy out lines that move you; draw character maps and plot timelines (like the ones on this page!).

Selective reading (选择性阅读) ▸

Read with a question in mind and pick only the parts that answer it — e.g. choose Fu Lei’s letters by topic.

How to read poems (如何读诗) ▸

Read aloud, find the images, feel the emotion. In Ai Qing’s poems “land” and “sun” keep coming back — ask why.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Standard: 《义务教育语文课程标准（2022年版）》, task group “whole-book reading” (整本书阅读); stage 4 asks students to read two or three classics a year and find their own reading methods, with at least 2.6 million characters of reading outside class.

Textbook (统编版): Gr 7 vol 1 — 朝花夕拾 (getting past the distance to a classic), 西游记 (close reading & skipping); Gr 7 vol 2 — 骆驼祥子 (marking & annotation), 海底两万里 (speed reading); Gr 8 vol 1 — 红星照耀中国 (reading non-fiction reportage), 昆虫记 (reading popular science); Gr 8 vol 2 — 傅雷家书 (selective reading), 钢铁是怎样炼成的 (copying & notes); Gr 9 vol 1 — 艾青诗选 (how to read poems), 水浒传 (reading classical novels); Gr 9 vol 2 — 儒林外史 (reading satire), 简·爱 (reading foreign novels).

Notes: copyright — for modern works (Lao She, Ai Qing, Fu Lei, Snow, Ostrovsky in translation) we use our own summaries only; the Jane Eyre line is from the public-domain English original. Stops and chapter numbers follow the standard 100-chapter Journey to the West; the route map is a sketch (示意图) and most places in the novel are fictional. Dates are the usual textbook ones; dates for the old novels are approximate.
