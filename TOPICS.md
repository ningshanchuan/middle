# Science Lab — Comprehensive Topic List & Build Specs

Aligned to the **Georgia Standards of Excellence (GSE)**: Grade 6 Earth Science (S6E1–S6E6), Grade 7 Life Science (S7L1–S7L5),
Grade 8 Physical Science (S8P1–S8P5), and Social Studies 6–7 Geography (SS6G, SS7G). Astronomy follows S6E1–S6E2.
Every app: bilingual EN/中文, 3D centerpiece, ≥1 extra simulation, quiz (10–12 Qs), sort game, memory hints, misconception callout, teacher notes.
Format per topic: **id** — standard — key concepts | 3D centerpiece | extra simulation | sort game

## 7. Math 数学 (26) — animated, intuition-first lessons (Georgia K-12 Mathematics Standards 2021)
Style: a dark animation canvas, smooth eased motion, colour-coded quantities, and narration that builds the WHY before the rule ("discover it, then name it"). Each lesson = MathAnim lesson (7–10 narrated steps) + 1–2 live "Explore" interactives + quiz (10–12) + sort + teacher notes. Reference: `math/pythagorean.html`.
Format: **id** — strand — the big idea / "aha" storyline | Explore interactive | sort game
1. **ratios** — 6.NR/6.PAR — a ratio compares two quantities that grow together; tape diagram → double number line → ratio table → unit rate ("how much for ONE?"); equivalent ratios as stretching both bars | recipe/paint mixer: drag to scale a ratio, double number line updates; unit-price comparison | sort: equivalent to 2:3 vs not.
2. **percents** — 6.NR/7.PAR — percent = "per 100": a 10×10 grid fills; benchmark 50%/25%/10%/1%; percent of a number by scaling; discounts/tax/percent increase as multiplying by (1 ± p) | percent bar model: drag part/whole/percent, solve any missing one; sale-price calculator | sort: fraction/decimal/percent matching groups.
3. **fraction-division** — 6.NR — "how many groups of ⅔ fit in 4?": bars cut into pieces animate fitting; leftover part counts as a fraction of a group; why invert-and-multiply works (common denominators → divide numerators) | fraction bar fitter with two fraction sliders | sort: quotient >1 vs <1.
4. **integers** — 6.NR — number line extends left (temperature, sea level, debt); opposites as reflection across 0; absolute value as distance; ordering | elevator/thermometer number line; compare-and-order game | sort: greater than −3 vs less.
5. **coordinate-plane** — 6.PAR — two number lines cross → address of a point; four quadrants via sign pairs; reflections flip signs; distance along grid lines | point-plotting game ("hit the target"), reflect a shape | sort: points by quadrant.
6. **expressions** — 6.PAR — a variable is a box/placeholder; writing expressions from words; like terms as same-shaped tiles; distributive property as area of a rectangle split into two; factoring as reverse | algebra-tile rectangle builder: a(b + c) ⇄ ab + ac | sort: equivalent to 3(x + 2) vs not.
7. **equations** — 6.PAR/7.PAR — an equation is a balance; do the same to both sides; one-step → two-step (undo in reverse order); inequalities as a range of solutions on a number line; flipping sign when multiplying by a negative | balance scale solver with draggable weights / x-boxes; inequality number line | sort: solution vs not a solution.
8. **area** — 6.GSR — rectangle area as rows × columns; parallelogram: cut a triangle, slide it → rectangle; triangle = half a parallelogram (two copies); trapezoid: two copies make a parallelogram | shape morpher with base/height sliders showing area stays the same when slanting (shear) | sort: formulas to shapes.
9. **surface-volume** — 6.GSR/7.GSR — volume = layers of unit cubes (base area × height); fractional edge lengths; surface area = unfold the net (3D unfolding with three.js or 2D animated net) | 3D box with L/W/H sliders: fill-with-cubes and unfold-net buttons | sort: measures of volume vs surface area (units², units³).
10. **statistics** — 6.DSR — dot plots/histograms; mean as the balance point (fair share leveling animation); median as the middle; spread (range, IQR, mean absolute deviation); outliers pull the mean | drag dots on a number line, watch mean/median/MAD update; box plot builder | sort: measures of center vs spread.
11. **proportional** — 7.PAR — y = kx: a straight line through the origin; k = unit rate = y/x for every point; tables, graphs and equations as three views; non-proportional comparisons | constant-of-proportionality slider with table + graph + equation linked | sort: proportional vs not (tables/graphs/stories).
