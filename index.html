Welcome to Genius Classroom

Lessons you do, not just read: spin 3D models, drag time sliders, explore maps, see real photos, run simulations and quiz yourself. Covers what middle-school students learn in both the US and mainland China: science, math, English, social studies and U.S. history (Georgia standards, grades 6–8), plus China’s history, geography, civics and Chinese (统编版 textbooks), and computer science, music and art.

📦 Source code on GitHub ↗

Aligned to the Georgia Standards of Excellence: Grade 6 Earth Science (S6E), Grade 7 Life Science (S7L), Grade 8 Physical Science (S8P), Social Studies 6–7 Geography (SS6G/SS7G), Georgia K-12 Mathematics Standards grades 6–8 (2021); China History, Geography & Civics follow mainland China's 2022 compulsory-education curriculum standards and the unified (部编版) textbooks. 3D models are simplified.
