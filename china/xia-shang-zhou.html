Xia, Shang & Zhou: Early States & the Bronze Age

Could you read a note written 3,000 years ago? In China, people still can. Meet China’s first three dynasties (ruling families): the Xia (夏 Xià), the Shang (商 Shāng) and the Western Zhou (西周 Xī Zhōu), about 2070–771 BCE — the age of Egypt’s pharaoh Tutankhamun and the Trojan War. Words you will need (click to open)

Dynasty 朝代 cháodài

A line of rulers from one family, and the time they ruled. China’s history is counted in dynasties, the way US history is counted in presidents.

Bronze 青铜 qīngtóng

Copper melted together with tin. New bronze shines like gold; the green colour comes from 3,000 years of rust.

Ding and zun 鼎 dǐng · 尊 zūn

A ding is a cooking cauldron on legs that became a holy vessel and a sign of power. A zun is a wine jar used in ceremonies.

Oracle-bone script 甲骨文 jiǎgǔwén

Writing that Shang kings had carved on turtle shells and ox bones when they asked the spirits questions. It is the earliest known mature Chinese writing.

Hereditary rule 世袭制 shìxí zhì

The throne passes from father to son (or brother). Before that, legend says leaders chose the most able person to follow them (禅让 shànràng, “abdication”).

Enfeoffment 分封制 fēnfēng zhì

The Zhou king handed out land and people to relatives and loyal generals, who ran their own small states and owed him soldiers and gifts. Europe’s feudal system, 1,800 years later, worked in a similar way.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Aligned with the 2022 Compulsory Education History Curriculum Standard, Ancient China, “Xia, Shang and Zhou” (in summary): know the succession of Xia, Shang and Western Zhou; understand enfeoffment; learn the achievements of early states through oracle-bone script and bronzes. Textbook: Unified edition, Chinese History, Grade 7 Vol. 1, Unit 2 (lessons on the founding of Xia, Shang rule, Western Zhou enfeoffment, and bronzes and oracle-bone script).

Ideas: (1) use the time slider to explain the name “bronze” (青铜: copper–tin alloy whose rust is green) as evidence work; (2) turn on “Compare size” and ask how many people it took to cast the ding: evidence of advanced Shang bronze casting; (3) after step 2 of the animation, debate whether hereditary rule was progress or regress (guide: it marks the birth of the early state).
