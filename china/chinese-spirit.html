One canal, two dates

One account dates the opening of the canal’s main channel to 1965. Opening record

A second account dates the completion of the full network, including its branch channels, to 1969. Completion record

How could you use both dates in one sentence?

Water began flowing through the main channel in 1965, and crews completed the full network in 1969. Each date makes sense when you say what happened that year.
