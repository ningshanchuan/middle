History of China · 1949–1976

How do you rebuild a country of 540 million people?

In 1949 China had more than three times as many people as the United States, but after a century of invasions and wars it made less steel in a year than the US made in a single day. On 1 October 1949 the People’s Republic of China (中华人民共和国 Zhōnghuá Rénmín Gònghéguó) — “New China” — was founded. Drag the time slider and see for yourself what happened next.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

This lesson follows China’s national curriculum (History Curriculum Standard for Compulsory Education, 2022 edition) and the national unified textbook Chinese History, Grade 8 Volume 2 — Unit 1 “The founding and consolidation of the People’s Republic” and Unit 2 “The establishment of the socialist system and the exploration of socialist construction”. Periodisation, terms and evaluations (including the textbook’s own account of the mistakes of the Great Leap Forward, the people’s commune movement and the “Cultural Revolution”) are the textbook’s. The English text adds pinyin and US/world anchors for American middle-schoolers; these anchors are not part of the Chinese textbook.

Suggestion: let students drag the slider and name the four stages aloud, then use the harvest chart to discuss why the line dips in 1959–1961 and recovers afterwards (the textbook’s lesson: respect objective economic laws and seek truth from facts). Harvest figures are rounded official statistics, shown for the trend only. Maps are schematic.
