👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports the 2022 Compulsory-Education Geography Curriculum Standard (“Understanding China”): use maps and data to describe the main features of China’s climate and how they affect life and work; describe the main features of the Yangtze and the Yellow River and their influence on society and the economy. Textbook: Grade 8 Vol. 1, Ch. 2 The Natural Environment of China, §2 Climate and §3 Rivers.

Ideas: ① drag the month from January to July and have students state the pattern (big north–south gap in winter, hot nearly everywhere in summer); ② on the Rain & winds layer press play and follow the rain belt: South China in May, the Yangtze in June (plum rains), North and Northeast China in July–August, retreat in September; ③ let students click a place and read off its temperature zone, wet/dry region and whether it is in the monsoon region; ④ compare the two river profiles: big drops upstream (hydropower), a gentle lower Yangtze (shipping), and the “above-ground” lower Yellow River. For US students, compare with the Mississippi and with the 100th-meridian dry line.
