China’s Territory & Administrative Divisions

Which is bigger: China or the United States? (Spoiler: it is almost a tie.) China sits in eastern Asia on the west coast of the Pacific, at about the same latitudes as the US. Instead of 50 states it has 34 provincial-level divisions. 🏛️ Is a province the same as a state?

Both are the first level below the national government. The big difference: China is a unitary state, so every division is led by the central government, while US states are part of a federal system and have their own constitutions. China’s 34 divisions come in four kinds:

  • 23 provinces (省 shěng): the ordinary kind
  • 5 autonomous regions (自治区 zìzhìqū): home regions of large ethnic minorities
  • 4 municipalities (直辖市 zhíxiáshì): giant cities that report straight to the central government, a bit like Washington, D.C. not belonging to any state
  • 2 special administrative regions (SARs): Hong Kong and Macao, under “one country, two systems”
🌅 One clock, two sunrises

China is about 5,200 km (3,200 miles) wide, farther than New York to San Francisco. The sun rises over the far east more than 4 hours before it reaches the Pamir Plateau in the far west. Yet the whole country uses Beijing Time, so in Xinjiang schools simply start later by the clock.

🚗 Where will you see the short names?

On every licence plate! A Beijing plate starts with 京 (Jīng), a Shanghai plate with 沪 (Hù), a Guangdong plate with 粤 (Yuè). Railways use them too: the 京沪 (Jīng–Hù) high-speed line runs from Beijing to Shanghai, the way “I-95” tells an American where a road goes.

💡 How Chinese students memorise all 34“两湖两广两河山，五江云贵福吉安，四西二宁青甘陕，还有内台北上天，港澳海南和重庆” — a Chinese rhyme that lists all 34 divisions in five lines (e.g. 两湖 = Hunan & Hubei, 五江 = Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Heilongjiang, Xinjiang).

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports the 2022 Compulsory-Education Geography Curriculum Standard, theme “Understanding China”: describe China’s location (hemispheres, land–sea position, latitude) and its advantages, know its territory, and locate the 34 provincial-level divisions with their short names and seats. Textbook: PEP Geography Grade 8 Vol. 1, Chapter 1 “China in the World”, Section 1 “Territory”.

Suggestions: (1) run the guided tour in order: location → area → neighbours → seas → divisions; (2) pairs quiz each other with the hidden-column table; (3) use the game’s “seat” mode as a class contest. The map is schematic, drawn from standard-map data, and includes Taiwan Province and the South China Sea Islands.
