Chinese painting & calligraphy: brush, ink and empty space

One brush, one ink stick, one sheet of paper — Chinese artists have worked with just these for over 2,000 years. In the Northern Song dynasty, 18-year-old Wang Ximeng painted a scroll almost 12 m (39 ft) long — longer than a US school bus! And calligraphers practise all eight basic strokes in one single character: 永 (yǒng, “forever”).

Your mission: write the Eight Principles of 永 in order, unroll real masterpiece scrolls the old way, carve your own seal, cut a symmetrical paper-cut, and fire a blue-and-white porcelain vase.

Mission 1: the Eight Principles of 永

The character 永 contains all eight basic strokes of regular script. Start at the green dot and follow the arrow to the red dot. Move slowly for a thicker, stronger line; the brush runs dry as you go and leaves streaky “flying white” (飞白). Get all eight right to build a whole 永.

🖌️ How do you hold a brush?

Hold the brush upright, control it with your fingers, and move your wrist and whole arm. Press briefly at the start of a stroke and turn the tip back at the end — like breathing in and out.

Mission 2: unroll a masterpiece the old way

A Chinese handscroll is never hung all at once. You unroll it from right to left, one arm’s length at a time, travelling through the landscape. Press ▶ Unroll or drag the picture; use zoom to see details.

    📊 How long are these scrolls?

    Data (cm, commonly published sizes; sources differ slightly): A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains ≈ 1191.5; Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains ≈ 636.9 (Master Wuyong scroll) + 51.4 (Remaining Mountain) — burned into two pieces in 1650; Along the River During the Qingming Festival ≈ 528.7; Feng Chengsu’s copy of the Orchid Pavilion Preface ≈ 69.9. A US school bus ≈ 12 m (40 ft).

    Mission 3: carve a seal & cut a paper window flower

    Seal carving (篆刻): characters are carved in reverse on stone, and stamping prints them in red. 朱文 (zhū wén) = red characters; 白文 (bái wén) = white characters on red. Paper-cutting (剪纸): fold red paper a few times, cut once, unfold — instant symmetry, the same rotation and mirror symmetry you meet in math.

      Mission 4: blue-and-white porcelain designer (main game)

      Jingdezhen potters painted designs with cobalt pigment on unfired clay, covered them with clear glaze, and fired them at about 1,300 °C (2,370 °F). Before firing, the cobalt looks dull gray-black — the brilliant blue only appears in the kiln! Choose a motif for each band, then fire the kiln.

        👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

        China: 《义务教育艺术课程标准（2022年版）》 Fine Arts, Stage 3 (grades 6–7) tasks “传承传统工艺” (traditional crafts such as paper-cutting and ceramics) and “领略世界美术的多样性”; competencies 文化理解、审美感知、艺术表现、创意实践. Junior-high art textbooks (人教版/人美版) have units on Chinese painting (landscape, bird-and-flower), calligraphy and seal carving, folk art (paper-cutting) and ceramics; Chinese and History classes (e.g. Grade 7 vol. 2, culture of the Song dynasty) also cover calligraphy and Song painting.

        US standards: Georgia Standards of Excellence for Visual Art (2017), grades 6–8: VA6–8.CR.1.c (inspiration from other cultures and times), VA6–8.CR.2.a (2D work in varied media), VA6–8.CR.3 (skill through practice and persistence), VA6–8.RE.1.a (time, place and culture in artworks), VA6–8.CN.1.a (universal themes across cultures). NCAS: VA:Cr1.2.7a, VA:Re7.2.7a, VA:Cn11.1.7a. Math link: reflection and rotation symmetry in paper-cuts (grade 7–8 transformations).

        Facts (common sources): the “Eight Principles of 永” are traditionally linked to Wang Xizhi (Eastern Jin) or the monk Zhiyong (Sui) — accounts differ. Chinese paper-cutting joined UNESCO’s Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2009. Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains was nearly burned in 1650; the front piece (剩山图) is in the Zhejiang Provincial Museum and the longer back piece (无用师卷) in the Taipei Palace Museum. Blue-and-white is usually fired at about 1,280–1,350 °C. The seal tool uses your device’s regular-script font, not a true seal-script font.
