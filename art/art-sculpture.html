Quick check · Form and making

Light reveals form

Side light brightens the surfaces turned toward it and darkens the ones turned away. Predict where the shadow will go, then drag the sun to find out.

Follow the edge of the shadow

Look inside the opening, then at the pedestal. The dark part of the form facing away from the light is in form shadow. A cast shadow falls where the sculpture blocks light from reaching another surface.

Which light makes the opening look deepest? Point to a change from light to dark as evidence.

Proportion and pose

Proportion is how the sizes of parts relate. As you change the stance, the head stays the same size. In contrapposto, most weight rests on one leg while the other relaxes. The hips and shoulders often tilt in opposite directions.

Freeze in each of the two stances. Which might show a character waiting? Then look for the supporting leg in David.

Measure in head heights

See a worked example

Given a figure 18 cm tall with a head 3 cm tall, how many head heights fit into its total height? Divide 18 cm by 3 cm to get 6 head heights.

Find the setting that makes the figure 4.5 head heights tall. Which proportion draws more attention to the head?

Teacher and family notes

Give learners quiet looking time, then ask them to point out one concrete detail. Different interpretations can all work when learners can explain them with evidence. A flashlight and a crumpled paper ball or clay form can reproduce the light activity.
