How do you read the staff?

Five lines, four spaces. In treble clef (𝄞) the lines bottom-to-top are E G B D F (“Every Good Boy Does Fine”) and the spaces spell F A C E. Bass clef (𝄢) lines are G B D F A (“Good Boys Do Fine Always”).

How do you read 简谱?

1 2 3 4 5 6 7 = do re mi fa sol la ti. A dot above raises a note an octave; a dot below lowers it. “1 = C” means 1 is sung on C; switch to “1 = G” and the same numbers move up to G. That is movable do.

What are the black keys called?

A sharp ♯ raises a note by a half step; a flat ♭ lowers it. So one black key can be C♯ or D♭. A natural ♮ cancels them.

Mission 2: build scales

A scale is a ladder of notes climbing from a home note (the tonic) in a fixed pattern of steps. Whole step (W) = two half steps; half step (H) = two neighbouring keys. Pick a tonic and a scale type, watch the steps, listen — and crack the challenges.

Chinese students usually learn 简谱 first; US students learn the staff — this lesson shows both side by side so students can “translate.”

    Notes: pitches use 12-tone equal temperament with A4 = 440 Hz (frequencies are exact calculations). 简谱 uses movable do; minor and pentatonic modes are written in their parent major key (A minor as 1 = C with 6 as the tonic). Voice and instrument ranges are approximate.
