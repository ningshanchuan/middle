Music Through Time & Cultures

If you time-travelled 400 years back, would you recognise the music? In 1977 humans bolted a Chinese guqin piece, 《流水》 “Flowing Streams,” onto the Voyager space probes — to aliens, that is one of the sounds of Earth.

Your mission: travel the timeline from 2,400-year-old bronze bells to hip-hop and film scores, remix one tune — “Twinkle, Twinkle” — into seven styles, then run the music museum and put every exhibit back in the right era.

🔊 “Style sketches” are original synthesized examples, not the real pieces; real recordings come from Wikimedia Commons (public domain or free licences).

    👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

    US standards: Georgia Standards of Excellence, Middle School General Music — MSGM6–8.CN.2 (connect music to history and culture: describe characteristics of genres and styles from various cultures; classify exemplary works by genre and style; compare music of world cultures, its functions, musicians and performance conditions), MSGM6–8.RE.1 (recognize characteristics of diverse genres and cultures). National Core Arts Standards: MU:Re7.2 (analyze music in context), MU:Cn11.0 (relate music to societal, cultural and historical contexts). Jazz, blues and hip-hop are American music created by African American musicians.

    China: Compulsory Education Arts Curriculum Standards (2022), Music, Stage 4 (Grades 7–9), appreciation — listening to Chinese traditional music (guqin, pipa pieces, opera) and representative works of other countries and periods, with their cultural background (the “cultural understanding” competency). Links to the national history textbooks: the Marquis Yi bells (Grade 7, pre-Qin culture), the birth of Peking opera (Anhui troupes enter Beijing, Grade 7 Volume 2, Ming–Qing culture), and 《义勇军进行曲》 / 《黄河大合唱》 (Grade 8 Volume 1, War of Resistance against Japan).

    Notes: period dates are conventional approximations, and composers often straddle two periods (Beethoven bridges Classical and Romantic). Style sketches and the seven “Twinkle” arrangements are original synthesized examples to show style features; real recordings use only public-domain or freely licensed files from Wikimedia Commons. 《高山流水》 is the pre-Qin “true friend” story; the guqin pieces 《高山》 and 《流水》 that survive today were first printed in the Ming-dynasty handbook 《神奇秘谱》 (1425).
