Rhythm & Beat

Why does your foot start tapping to a song before you even decide to? Your brain hunts for the beat automatically. Rhythm is the pattern you build on top of it.

How drummers count

What is “1 e & a”?

Split each beat into four: 1–e–&–a (“one-ee-and-uh”). Eighth notes land on “1” and “&”; sixteenths use all four. Chinese teachers often say 哒 (da), 哒哒, 哒啦哒啦 for the same rhythms.

What does a dot do?

A dot adds half the note’s value: dotted half = 2 + 1 = 3 beats; dotted quarter = 1 + ½ = 1½ beats. 简谱 uses dots too: “5·” is a dotted quarter.

Is a rest part of the music?

Yes! A rest is silence with a length. Every note value has a matching rest. In 简谱 a rest is written “0”.

⚠️ Don’t mix up:The beat is the steady pulse, like a clock’s tick. The rhythm is the pattern of long and short sounds. One beat can carry endless rhythms.

    👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

    US standards: Georgia Standards of Excellence, Middle School General Music — MSGM6–8.PR.3 (read and notate music: identify and use standard notation symbols; read rhythms in various meters and note values), MSGM6–8.CR.1 (improvise simple rhythmic and melodic variations), MSGM6–8.RE.1 (describe music with appropriate terminology). National Core Arts Standards: MU:Pr4.2 (analyze structure and elements such as rhythm and meter), MU:Cr2.1 (use notation to document musical ideas).

    China: Compulsory Education Arts Curriculum Standards (2022), Music, Stage 4 (Grades 7–9) — musical elements such as rhythm, meter and tempo within the practices of performing and creating; fits the learning tasks on instrumental performance and creating/presenting. Both countries’ middle-school music asks students to read time signatures, note values and tempo words — this lesson puts staff rhythms and 简谱 rhythms side by side.

    Notes: drum sounds are synthesized, not recordings. Tap-back scoring: within 60 ms = Perfect, 120 ms = Great, 200 ms = OK. 板眼 and the 哒 counting syllables are common classroom usage. Tempo ranges are approximate.
