Exponents: count the factors

In round 1, you share a video with 2 friends. At every round, each person from the previous round shares it with 2 more people. If every share reaches a different new viewer, how many new viewers are there in round 20? A positive whole-number exponent counts copies of the base used as factors. Expand the powers, extend the pattern to zero and negative exponents, and use powers of ten to calculate with very large or very small quantities.

⚠️ Common mix-up:3⁴ is not 3 × 4 = 12; it is 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81. When you multiply powers of the same base you ADD the exponents (2³ · 2⁴ = 2⁷), you don’t multiply them. A negative exponent on a nonzero base gives the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power (2⁻³ = 1/8).

Calculate with scientific notation

For addition and subtraction, match the powers of ten first. For multiplication and division, work with the coefficients and powers separately. Then write the result in scientific notation.

How does a calculator show scientific notation?

A calculator may use E for “times ten raised to”. For example, 1.56E9 means 1.56 × 10⁹.

Practice · Combine two measurements

One piece of thin wire is 0.00032 m long and another is 8 × 10⁻⁵ m long. Find the combined length.

Solution: express both lengths using the same power of ten, then add them.

Answer: the combined length is 4 × 10⁻⁴ m.

Practice · Add, then divide

Given: two files take up 3.6 × 10⁶ bytes and 9 × 10⁵ bytes. The transfer rate is 1.5 × 10⁵ bytes per second.

Find: the total time to transfer both files, in seconds.

Solution: add the two file sizes and divide by the transfer rate.

Answer: 30 seconds.
