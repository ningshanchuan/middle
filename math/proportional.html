Proportional Relationships: why a line through the origin?

You earn $12 for every lawn you mow. How much for 7 lawns, and what would the graph look like? Walking at a steady pace, paying by the pound, earning by the hour: “the same amount for each one” is everywhere. We start with a table, watch it become a line, then a tiny equation, y = kx.

⚠️ Common mix-up:A straight line does NOT automatically mean proportional! y = 2x + 4 is a straight line too, but it misses the origin and y/x keeps changing. Proportional means BOTH: a straight line AND it passes through (0, 0).

Explore: table, graph and equation, linked

Drag the slider (or the orange handle on the graph) to change the constant k. Then add a “starting amount” b and watch what happens to the ratios y/x and to the line.

Advanced: inverse variation y = c / x (purple curve)

Direct variation keeps the ratio y/x fixed. Inverse variation keeps the product x·y fixed: double x and y is cut in half.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 7.PAR (decide whether two quantities are proportional; identify the constant of proportionality (unit rate) in tables, graphs, equations and verbal descriptions; represent with y = kx; explain what (0, 0) and (1, k) mean on the graph). After step 2, ask students to predict what the points will look like on a graph. Step 8 (the taxi) targets the misconception “any straight line is proportional”. In Explore, have students raise b from 0 and watch the y/x column go from all-equal to all-different.
