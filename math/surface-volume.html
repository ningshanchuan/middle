Surface Area & Volume: fill it or wrap it?

How much does a box hold, and how much paper wraps it? Two very different questions. First we fill a box with little cubes, layer by layer; then we cut it open and lay it flat to see exactly where volume and surface area come from.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Volume counts the cubes that FILL the inside (cubic units, cm³); surface area counts the squares that COVER the outside (square units, cm²). The two numbers can look similar, but they measure completely different things.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 6.GSR / 7.GSR (volume of right rectangular prisms by packing unit cubes, including fractional edge lengths; surface area from nets; real-world problems). Before steps 3–4, have students predict “how many in one layer, how many layers”, so V = l × w × h is understood as base area × number of layers. Steps 6–7 show a ½-unit cube has volume ⅛, so the formula still works for fractional edges. The Explore challenge leads to the idea that, for a fixed volume, the more cube-like the box, the smaller its surface area.
