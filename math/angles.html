Angle relationships: turn and see

When a skateboarder lands a “360”, how much did they actually turn? An angle measures an amount of turn. Starting from that one idea you will see why vertical angles are equal, why parallel lines cut by a transversal make only two different angles, and why the angles of every triangle add up to 180°.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Complementary angles add to 90°, supplementary to 180° (C comes before S, just as 90 comes before 180). And corresponding or alternate interior angles are equal only when the two lines are parallel.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 7.GSR / 8.GSR (grade 7: use complementary, supplementary, vertical and adjacent angle facts to write and solve equations; grade 8: triangle angle sum, exterior angles, and angles formed when parallel lines are cut by a transversal). Before step 4, ask students to predict which angles stay equal as one line turns. The “slide the crossing” in step 5 is the key idea behind corresponding angles: parallel lines point in exactly the same direction. Step 8 proves the triangle sum with a parallel line through a vertex, reusing alternate interior angles. Have students tear the corners off a paper triangle to check.
