Expressions: one rectangle, two ways to count it

Three friends each buy a movie ticket and a $2 drink. Can you write the total before you even know the ticket price? A letter is just a box that holds a number. Draw it as a tile, and combining like terms, the distributive property and factoring all become tile-arranging. You can see exactly why 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6.

⚠️ Common mix-up:3(x + 2) is NOT 3x + 2! The 3 multiplies every term inside, so it is 3x + 6. And x + 3 can't be combined into 3x: a bar and a square are not like terms.
Memory hint“The number outside shakes hands with everyone inside”: a shakes hands with b, then with c, giving ab + ac.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 6.PAR (using variables, writing expressions from words, evaluating, and generating equivalent expressions with properties of operations, including the distributive property and factoring out a common factor). Algebra tiles (bar = x, square = 1) make “like terms” concrete: only matching shapes combine. Before step 5, have students compute 3 × 6 mentally, then ask whether it can be split into two easier pieces. At step 6 stress that the bar length is arbitrary, so the identity holds for every x. The “Factor it” activity leads naturally to the greatest common factor.
