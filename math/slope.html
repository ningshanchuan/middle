Slope: how steep, in one number

Which is steeper, the wheelchair ramp at your school or a ski run? How do you put a number on “steep”? How steep is a line, exactly? Walk along it in little staircase steps and you will find that “rise ÷ run” is the same for every step. That unchanging ratio is the slope, and together with a starting value it gives y = mx + b.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Slope is rise ÷ run (vertical change ÷ horizontal change), not the other way round. Subtract the y’s and the x’s in the same order. And keep these apart: a horizontal line has slope 0, but a vertical line’s slope is undefined (you cannot divide by 0).

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 7.PAR (proportional relationships, unit rate) and 8.FGR (using similar triangles to explain why slope is the same between any two points; y = mx + b; finding slope and intercept from two points or a table). Steps 3–4 are the heart: ask students to predict whether the big and small triangles have the same rise/run before playing. Note that Chinese textbooks usually write y = kx + b, where k plays the role of m. In Explore, have students describe what happens as m passes from positive through zero to negative; the matching game practises finding slope from two points.
