Circles: where does π come from?

Is one 16-inch pizza more food than two 8-inch pizzas? Roll a wheel once and count how many diameters it travels. Then slice a circle into thin pieces and rebuild it as a rectangle. The formulas C = πd and A = πr² will appear on their own.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Circumference C = 2πr is a length (cm); area A = πr² is an area (cm²). Area uses the radius: a circle with diameter 10 cm has area π × 5² ≈ 78.5 cm², not π × 10².

    👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

    Supports Georgia 7.GSR (know and use the formulas for the circumference and area of a circle; give an informal derivation of the relationship between them). Before step 2, ask students to estimate how far the wheel travels in one turn (most underestimate). Step 4 connects to scale drawings: all circles are similar, so C ÷ d is constant. Have groups cut paper circles into sectors and rearrange them, then confirm with the Explore slider. For the measuring activity, students can bring round objects, measure with string and a ruler, and compare their real data with the simulation.
