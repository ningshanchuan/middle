Algebraic Expressions & Polynomials

A row of squares shares sides. Can you write one expression that gives the number of matchsticks for a row of any length?

From a pattern to an expression

Arrangement
3

Look at the first shape, then at how many sticks each new shape adds. Write the total number of sticks in terms of n.

Use whole numbers, n, +, −, × or *, and parentheses. You can write a coefficient directly before n.

See the rule and substitute

One expression can give the stick count for a whole row because n can stand for any allowed number of shapes. To evaluate, replace n with the given number and follow the order of operations.

Worked example · Write and evaluate from words

A bike rental costs $5 up front and $3 per hour. Let h be the number of hours. What does a 4-hour rental cost?

  1. Write the expression. Add the fixed fee and the hourly charge.
    5 + 3h (USD)
  2. Substitute and evaluate. Multiply before adding.
    $5 + ($3/h)(4 h)
    = $5 + $12 = $17

Put a negative input in parentheses. When x = −2, x² = (−2)² = 4.

A monomial can be a number, a variable, or a number multiplied by powers of variables with whole-number exponents. The number factor, including its sign, is the coefficient. For a nonzero monomial, add the variable exponents to find its degree.

A polynomial is a sum of one or more monomials. Each monomial in the sum is a term. A term without a variable is a constant term. After combining like terms, the degree of a nonzero polynomial is the highest degree among its remaining terms.

Sort algebraic expressions

Sort one-term polynomials into the monomial group and polynomials with two or more terms into the next group. Drag a card or tap it and then tap its group.

Notation notes · Exponents, constants, and zero

An unwritten exponent on a variable is 1. A nonzero constant has degree 0. The zero polynomial has no assigned degree in this lesson. The English term polynomial covers both mainland Chinese categories of 整式 (zhěngshì), single terms (单项式, dānxiàngshì) and sums of multiple terms (多项式, duōxiàngshì).

Like terms have the same variables raised to the same powers. All constant terms are like terms. Add the coefficients and keep the variable part unchanged. A positive tile and a negative tile of the same type form a zero pair.

Memory tipMatch the variable parts first. Then add the signed coefficients.
Worked example · Like terms with two variables
5ab² − 3a²b − 2b²a

Rewrite b²a as ab² to see that the first and third terms match.

= (5 − 2)ab² − 3a²b
= 3ab² − 3a²b

The terms ab² and a²b both have degree 3. Compare the exponent on each individual variable to decide whether they combine.

Remove brackets to add or subtract

Sign before the brackets
1/3

Sign ruleA plus keeps every sign inside the brackets. A minus reverses every sign inside. Then combine like terms.

Subtracting a group multiplies every term inside by −1, so −(a − b) = −a + b. The coefficient of a is 1, and the coefficient of −a is −1.

Worked example · Difference of two polynomials

Simplify the expression.

(4x² − x + 1) − (2x² − 3x + 5)
  1. Remove brackets. Reverse every sign in the second polynomial.
    = 4x² − x + 1 − 2x² + 3x − 5
  2. Group and combine like terms.
    = (4 − 2)x² + (−1 + 3)x + (1 − 5)
    = 2x² + 2x − 4

Worked example · Given, find, solution, answer

Given A = 3x² − 2x + 1, B = x² + x − 4

Find The value of A − 3B when x = −2.

Solution Substitute the expressions for A and B. Then distribute and combine like terms.

A − 3B = (3x² − 2x + 1) − 3(x² + x − 4)
= 3x² − 2x + 1 − 3x² − 3x + 12
= −5x + 13
x = −2: −5(−2) + 13 = 10 + 13 = 23

Answer When x = −2, A − 3B equals 23.

In Advanced, make a polynomial stay constant and try substituting for a whole group.

Make the value independent of x

Adjust a and b so the result stays the same for every x. Then change c to see what happens to the fixed value.

ax² + bx + 7 − (2x² − 3x + c)
= (a − 2)x² + (b + 3)x + (7 − c)

Why do both coefficients have to vanish?

Compare x = 1 and x = −1. The squared terms match, so equal values force the linear coefficient b + 3 to be zero. Now compare x = 0 and x = 1 to see that the quadratic coefficient a − 2 must also be zero. With both coefficients zero, 7 − c is all that remains for every x.

Substitute for a whole group

Suppose you know the value of x − 2y. Rewrite −2x + 4y as −2(x − 2y). Now you can substitute for the whole group x − 2y and find the result.

3(x − 2y)² − 2x + 4y + 1
= 3(x − 2y)² − 2(x − 2y) + 1

Practice and quiz

Practice 1 · Find a side from the perimeter

A triangle has perimeter (8x + 3) cm. Two sides are (3x − 1) cm and (2x + 5) cm. Write an expression for the third side, then find its length at x = 3.

Show the steps

Subtract the sum of the two known sides from the perimeter.

[(8x + 3) − (3x − 1) − (2x + 5)] cm
= (8x + 3 − 3x + 1 − 2x − 5) cm
= (3x − 1) cm
x = 3: [3(3) − 1] cm = 8 cm
Practice 2 · Distribute, then substitute a negative number

Simplify and then evaluate at x = −3.

2(x² − 3x) − (2x² − x + 4)
Show the steps
= 2x² − 6x − 2x² + x − 4
= −5x − 4
x = −3: −5(−3) − 4 = 15 − 4 = 11
Practice 3 · Find the degree after simplifying
(2a²b − 3ab + 5) − (2ba² + ab − 1)
Show the steps
= 2a²b − 3ab + 5 − 2a²b − ab + 1
= −4ab + 6

The degree-three terms cancel. The highest-degree term left is −4ab, with degree 1 + 1 = 2.
