Check the pictures with the distributive property

The pictures use a > b > 0 so all lengths are positive. Expanding with the distributive property shows that the identities hold for all real a and b.

(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-ab+ab-b^2=a^2-b^2 (a+b)^2=a^2+ab+ab+b^2=a^2+2ab+b^2 (a-b)^2=a^2-ab-ab+b^2=a^2-2ab+b^2
Mental math · 99² and 102 × 98

Write 99 as 100 minus 1 and square the difference.

99^2=(100-1)^2 =100^2-2\cdot100\cdot1+1^2 =10{,}000-200+1=9{,}801

The factors are each 2 away from 100, on opposite sides. Use the difference of squares.

102\cdot98=(100+2)(100-2) =100^2-2^2=10{,}000-4=9{,}996
Common mix-up (a+b)^2\ne a^2+b^2 in general. With both a and b equal to 1, the left side is 4 and the right side is 2. The square-of-a-sum model shows the two missing rectangles of area ab.

Divide by a monomial

Find the missing widths

Both tiles have height 3x. Divide each tile’s area by its height to find its width. Subtract the width of a pink tile.

The picture uses positive x to give positive lengths. The algebra works for any nonzero x because the divisor is 3x. When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the exponents.

\frac{x^m}{x^n}=x^{m-n}\quad(x\ne0,\ m\ge n)

Here m and n are positive integers with m ≥ n, so the result is a polynomial.

Worked example · Divide by a negative monomial

Given x\ne 0
Find \frac{12x^3-8x^2+4x}{-4x}

Solution \frac{12x^3}{-4x}+\frac{-8x^2}{-4x}+\frac{4x}{-4x} =-3x^{3-1}+2x^{2-1}-1=-3x^2+2x-1

Answer -3x^2+2x-1

Check by multiplying the quotient by the divisor. (-4x)(-3x^2+2x-1)=12x^3-8x^2+4x

Enrichment Yang Hui’s triangle and powers of a binomial

Row zero has just one 1. Every row starts and ends with 1, and each number inside is the sum of the two above it. Row n gives the coefficients of the expansion, starting with the highest power of a.

In English it is often called Pascal’s triangle. Yang Hui recorded the pattern, and it also appears in Zhu Shijie’s Jade Mirror of the Four Unknowns from 1303.
