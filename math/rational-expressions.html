What is a rational expression?

In English, a rational expression is one polynomial divided by another. Its denominator cannot be an expression that is always zero. The Chinese textbook term 分式 (fēnshì) also requires a variable in the denominator. Exclude every value that makes the original denominator zero, even after reducing.

The basic property of rational expressions

Split every piece into the same number of smaller pieces. The shaded length stays the same. Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same polynomial. The fraction keeps its value wherever that factor is nonzero.

To reduce (约分, yuēfēn), divide out a common factor. Let x count the smaller pieces made from each piece of the top bar. In the diagram x is a positive integer. The equation below holds for every nonzero real x.

Use equal-sized pieces

Finding a common denominator (通分, tōngfēn) gives the fractions equal-sized pieces. Split a half and a third into x smaller pieces each and take one small piece from each. The denominators are now 2x and 3x, so use 6x as their least common denominator. Here 6 is the least common multiple of 2 and 3.

Operation rules

Multiply

Multiply the numerators and the denominators. Factor first so common factors can cancel. Each original denominator must be nonzero.

Divide

Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor. Both original denominators and the divisor must be nonzero.

Add and subtract

Find a common denominator first. Keep it and combine the numerators. When subtracting, put parentheses around the entire second numerator.

Negative integer exponents

A negative exponent calls for a reciprocal. For a fractional base, flip the entire base before raising it to the positive power.

Solve rational equations and check the roots

A rational equation has an unknown in a denominator. Exclude values that make an original denominator zero. Multiply every term on both sides by the least common denominator, then solve the resulting equation. Substitute each candidate into the original equation. If a candidate makes a denominator zero, it is an extraneous solution (增根, zēnggēn).

Work rates: split one job

Given that A can finish the job alone in 6 hours, how long would B take alone? Each worker keeps the same rate alone or together. Move the slider to change the time they take together.

A’s share B’s share

Count the whole job as 1. Let x be B’s solo time in hours, with x > 0. A does 1/6 of the job per hour and B does 1/x per hour. Multiply each rate by the time together in hours. The two shares add up to one job.

Travel rates: different distances, equal times

A cyclist rides 12 km out and returns by an 18 km route. The average speed on the way back is 3 km/h faster. Both rides take the same time. Find the average speed on the way out.

Show steps

Let v be the average speed on the way out in km/h, with v > 0. The average speed on the way back is v + 3 km/h. Divide distance by average speed to get time in hours on each side.

The candidate v = 6 is positive and gives an average return speed of 9 km/h. Both times are 2 h since 12 ÷ 6 = 18 ÷ 9 = 2. The average speed on the way out is 6 km/h.
