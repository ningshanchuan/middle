Explore standard form

Completing the square & the discriminant

Write the output y as f(x). Completing the square gives the vertex (h, k).

When Δ is positive, zero, or negative, the graph meets the x-axis at two points, one point, or no points. Challenge: start with “Two roots,” then change only c to make Δ = 0.

The two sides of the parabola mirror each other across its axis. When it opens up, y falls as x increases to the left of the vertex and rises to the right. When it opens down, the pattern reverses. Set x = 0 to find the y-intercept. Solve y = 0 to find the x-intercepts. Changing only c moves the whole graph up or down by the same amount.

Common mix-upChanging b moves the axis and can change the vertex height too. For a purely horizontal shift, change h in vertex form.

Worked example · From equation to graph

Given y = 2x² − 8x + 6, find the vertex, axis and x-intercepts. Then find the x-values for which y ≥ 0.

  1. Complete the square to read the vertex and axis.
  2. Set y = 0 and factor to find the two intercepts.
  3. The parabola opens up and lies below the x-axis between its roots. Include both zeros in the answer.

Use the vertex to optimize

Find the allowed inputs first. If both endpoints are allowed, compare their outputs with the vertex’s output if the vertex is in the domain. If an endpoint is excluded, the function may approach an extreme value without ever reaching it.

Fence against a wall · Maximum area

Use all 24 m of fencing on three sides of a rectangle beside a wall. Drag the point to change the depth x in meters and see how the area A in square meters changes. Open circles show the limits where no rectangle forms.

Worked solution · What dimensions give the largest area?

Given 24 m of fencing and a wall for the fourth side, find the dimensions and area of the largest rectangle.

  1. The two depths are x meters each, leaving (24 − 2x) meters along the wall. Both dimensions must be positive.
  2. In vertex form, the term −2(x − 6)² reaches its largest value, 0, at x = 6. That is where the area is largest.
  3. Answer: 6 m deep by 12 m along the wall gives a maximum area of 72 m².

Worked solution · When does it peak and land?

Given the height model above and a launch from the ground, find the peak height and when the ball reaches it, then find the landing time.

  1. The vertex form opens down, so the vertex gives the peak.
  2. Set height to zero to find the launch and landing times.
  3. Answer: The ball reaches 20 m at 2 s and lands at 4 s. The model’s time domain here is 0 to 4 s.

Find the rule from the vertex and another point

A parabola has vertex (−2, 3) and passes through (0, −5). Find the equation and determine whether y rises or falls as x increases for x > −2.

Show steps

Use h = −2 and k = 3 in vertex form, then substitute (0, −5) to find a.

The parabola opens down, so y decreases as x increases to the right of its axis x = −2.

When the vertex is outside the domain

In the earlier fence model, a narrower site limits the depth to 2 ≤ x ≤ 5 in meters. Find the largest and smallest possible areas.

Show steps

The vertex is at x = 6. From 2 to 5, larger x is closer to 6, making A = 72 − 2(x − 6)² larger. Compare the endpoints.

Answer: 70 m² is the maximum at x = 5 m. The minimum is 40 m² at x = 2 m.

Study notes & curriculum alignment

Change only c first and compare the number of x-intercepts. Then hold a fixed in vertex form and vary h or k one at a time. In an application, the vertex’s input tells when or where the extremum occurs, and its output gives the extreme value.
