Congruent figures have the same shape and size. Translations, rotations and reflections preserve lengths and angles. Triangles can face different ways and still be congruent if these motions make them coincide.

In △ABC ≅ △A′B′C′, list the matching vertices in the same order. A matches A′, B matches B′ and C matches C′. Matching sides have equal lengths, and matching angles have equal measures.

Reflection and line symmetry

Two figures are reflections of each other if folding across a line makes them coincide. A figure has line symmetry if folding makes it coincide with itself. The fold line is the axis of symmetry.

Two ways to work backward

A point equally distant from the ends of a segment lies on its perpendicular bisector. Inside an angle, a point equally distant from its two sides lies on the angle bisector.

Equilateral and equiangular

An isosceles triangle has equal base angles. Conversely, equal angles in a triangle have equal opposite sides. An equilateral triangle has three 60° angles and three lines of symmetry. A triangle with three equal angles is equilateral. An isosceles triangle with a 60° angle is also equilateral.

Find the two base angles

Given AB = AC and ∠A = 38° in △ABC.

Find ∠B and ∠C.

Show steps

Equal sides have equal opposite angles, so ∠B = ∠C. The interior angles of a triangle sum to 180°.

∠B = ∠C = (180° − 38°) ÷ 2 = 71°

Both base angles are 71°.

Use a perpendicular bisector

Given P on the perpendicular bisector of AB, PA = (2x + 1) cm and PB = (3x − 4) cm.

Find x and the length PA.

Show steps

The perpendicular-bisector property gives PA = PB, so the two measurements in centimeters are equal.

2x + 1 = 3x − 4 ⇒ x = 5

PA = (2 × 5 + 1) cm = 11 cm

The value of x is 5, and PA is 11 cm.

Turn AAS into ASA

Given ∠A = ∠D = 53°, ∠C = ∠F = 82° and AB = DE = 7 cm in △ABC and △DEF.

Prove △ABC ≅ △DEF.

Show proof

Use the triangle angle sum to find ∠B and ∠E.

∠B = ∠E = 180° − 53° − 82° = 45°

AB is the side between ∠A and ∠B. DE is the side between ∠D and ∠E. Therefore △ABC ≅ △DEF by ASA.

Check the reasoning

SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS and HL each guarantee congruence. SSA can give two noncongruent triangles. With only two or three equal angle pairs, triangles can differ in size. With only two equal side pairs, the included angles can differ.
