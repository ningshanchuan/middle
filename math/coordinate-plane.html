The Coordinate Plane: an address for every point

How does a video game know exactly where your character is standing on the screen? One number pins down a spot on a number line. How many numbers does a point on a flat page need? Cross two number lines and you get a map that can locate any point exactly.

⚠️ Common mix-up:In the ordered pair (x, y) the x-coordinate always comes first. (2, 5) and (5, 2) are different points! Also, a point on an axis, like (0, 4), is not in any quadrant.
Memory hint"Walk the hallway, then take the elevator": move across along x first, then up or down for y.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia 6.PAR (plotting points in all four quadrants, using signs to reason about location and reflections, and finding distances between points that share an x- or y-coordinate). Pause at step 2 and ask: “If one number isn't enough, how many do we need?” Before step 7, have students predict the folded coordinates. The plotting game gives a special hint when x and y are swapped, which is a good opening to discuss what “ordered” pair means.
