Worked example · Cut away two strips

Given a square with side length 7 units, cut a strip 3 units wide from the right and the bottom.

Find the area of the square that remains.

  1. Choose (a − b)² and set a = 7 and b = 3. The first strip has area 7 × 3 = 21 square units.
  2. The 3 by 3 corner went with the first strip. The second strip has only (7 − 3) × 3 = 12 square units left.
  3. The remaining area is 16 square units.
