Mainland textbooks write a linear function (一次函数, yīcì hánshù) as . Here k is the slope and b is the y-intercept. US textbooks often write , with m for the slope. If b = 0 and the slope is nonzero, the rule is a direct proportion and its graph passes through the origin.

In a direct proportion, the ratio y/x equals the constant of proportionality k for every nonzero x. Plot (0, 0) and (1, k). For , plot (0, b) and (1, k + b). Then draw the straight line through the points.

Use k for direction and b for the vertical crossing. Positive k rises to the right and negative k falls to the right.
Slope zero gives the horizontal line y = b, a constant function. US middle-school courses commonly include it among linear functions. The mainland definition of 一次函数 requires k ≠ 0.

Why does the line visit those quadrants?

A rising line comes from III and heads to I. If b is positive, it crosses the x-axis before the y-axis and also passes through II. If b is negative, it also passes through IV. A falling line goes from II to IV, with I added for positive b or III for negative b. When b = 0, only the two opposite quadrants remain.

Points on the axes are outside all four quadrants.

What if the two x-coordinates are equal?

Two distinct points with the same x-coordinate c determine the vertical line x = c. That input has several outputs, so the line does not define y as a function of x. If the two points coincide, a single point leaves infinitely many possible lines.

What if k is zero in kx + b = 0?

If b = 0, the line is the x-axis and every real x is a solution. If b is nonzero, the horizontal line misses the x-axis and the equation has no solution.

Enrichment · The boundary of a two-variable inequality

The solutions of y > 2x − 2 are ordered pairs (x, y). A point satisfies the inequality when its y-value is higher than the line at the same x. Use a dashed boundary for > or <. Use a solid boundary for ≥ or ≤ because equality is included.
