The Pythagorean Theorem: why a² + b² = c²?

A “65-inch” TV is not 65 inches wide. So what is 65 inches long? You may have memorized the formula. But why must it be true? This animation uses nothing but four identical triangles and one big square to let you see the answer.

⚠️ Common mix-up:This only works for RIGHT triangles, and c must be the hypotenuse, the longest side opposite the right angle.

Advanced: the diagonal of a box (Pythagoras in 3D)
