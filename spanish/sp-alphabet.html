Vowels and consonants

Five vowel sounds

Hold each vowel steady when you say it by itself. For a, i and u, try the vowels in Mandarin pinyin “mā,” “mǐ” and “mù.” For e and o, start near the first part of pinyin “ei” and “ou” and hold that sound. Listen and compare.

a, e, i, o, u

SpellingSoundHear an example

In gue and gui, the g sounds like the start of gato and the u is silent. The two dots in güe or güi tell you to pronounce the u.

guitarra, pingüino

Common mix-up The letter names be and uve differ, but b and v follow the same pronunciation rules in everyday words.

Stress, accents and cognates

Find the stressed syllable

Say the stressed syllable a little more prominently. Use the table to find the stress in words of two or more syllables.

Spelling clueStress and example

Any vowel can take a written accent (á, é, í, ó, ú). Direct questions and exclamations use marks at both ends. ¿Cómo se escribe? ¡Hola!

Try país. How many syllables does it have? Where is the stress?

Show the answer

It has two syllables, pa-ís, and the stress is on ís. The accent on í tells you to say the vowels in separate syllables.

Use a familiar word as a clue

Cognates share an origin. These Spanish words resemble English words and have a similar meaning. A false friend can look familiar but point to a different meaning.

Common mix-up Embarazada means pregnant. Éxito means success. Salida means exit.
