Memory tipSay Soy de and picture the place on the map.

Common mix-upPaís means “country,” and capital means “capital.” In La capital de Perú es Lima. the country comes after de.

A capital and a seat of government

Bolivia’s constitutional capital is Sucre. The national government is based in La Paz. A map of capitals marks Sucre, and a trip to see the legislature takes you to La Paz.

Spanish in the U.S. and Hispanic Heritage Month

September 15 to October 15

National Hispanic Heritage Month honors Hispanic and Latino communities and their contributions in the United States. It begins on September 15, the independence anniversary shared by Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, and Nicaragua.

Spanish in the United States

Among U.S. residents age five and older, Spanish was the most common language spoken at home after English, according to U.S. Census Bureau data for 2019. You may also hear it on local radio or see it on signs and menus.

Art

Las Meninas

Diego Velázquez painted a scene at the Spanish court in 1656. Follow the light through the room, from the young princess Margarita to the doorway and the mirror beyond her.

Museo del Prado · Painting Museo del Prado · Guide and film link

Frida Kahlo

In her self-portraits, the Mexican painter often made clothing, plants, and animals part of the picture. Her blue house in Mexico City is now a museum.

Museo Frida Kahlo · The artist

Guernica

Pablo Picasso painted it in 1937 in response to the bombing of the Basque town of Gernika during the Spanish Civil War. The sharp shapes and shades of gray express grief.

Museo Reina Sofía · Painting

Architecture

La Alhambra

At this palace and fortress in Granada, Spain, water and repeated patterns give the courtyards a quiet rhythm.

UNESCO · Granada

Sagrada Família

Look for the crowded stone figures on Antoni Gaudí’s Nativity façade in Barcelona. The façade and crypt are part of the UNESCO-listed Works of Antoni Gaudí.

UNESCO · Works of Antoni Gaudí

Machu Picchu

In Peru’s Andes, Inca buildings and terraces follow the shape of a mountain ridge.

UNESCO · Machu Picchu

Music and dance

Flamenco

A tradition rooted in southern Spain that brings singing, dancing and instrumental playing together. Listen for how the dancer’s steps respond to the song and guitar.

UNESCO · Flamenco Andalusian Flamenco Institute · Film

Mariachi

Mexican mariachi ensembles often combine voices, violins, guitars and trumpets. Listen to how the bright trumpet sound stands out against the strings.

▶ UNESCO · Mariachi

Salsa

Salsa draws on Afro-Cuban rhythms. Musicians in cities such as New York helped shape the sound, and Cali, Colombia, is known for its salsa dancing.

Smithsonian · The musical journey of salsa Smithsonian · Salsa in Cali

Tango

Tango grew in Buenos Aires and Montevideo, on either side of the Río de la Plata. As dance partners step, they shift their weight together.

UNESCO · Tango

Remembering and sharing food

Día de los Muertos

From late October into early November, many Mexican communities remember loved ones who have died. An ofrenda may hold photos, candles, flowers and favorite foods. Papel picado, or cut-paper banners, may hang nearby.

Smithsonian · Day of the Dead UNESCO · Day of the Dead traditions

Regional dishes

In Spain, Valencian paella is a rice dish cooked in a wide pan. In Peru, ceviche is often prepared with fish, citrus juice, onion and chili. Recipes vary by place.
