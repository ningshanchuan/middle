You can use different forms of “you” when talking with a friend or in a formal setting. Look at the table and listen for what changes.

AddressWhen to use itExample

Usage varies by region and relationship. In parts of Latin America, people often use vos with one person in familiar settings. The usual plural form is ustedes. In most of Spain, vosotros is the familiar plural “you” for a male or mixed group, and vosotras is used for an all-female group.

Common mix-up Keep the forms together. Use tú with estás and usted with está.

Memory tip Write the day number first, then de, then the month, as in el 5 de mayo.

Days and months normally begin with lowercase letters. For the first of the month, el primero de mayo is common in Latin America.
