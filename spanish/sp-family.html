Say whose it is

Put the possessive before the noun. Match the number of the noun that follows, such as one cousin or several cousins. Nuestro also changes with the noun’s gender.

No tengo hermanos.I don’t have any siblings.

Memory tipHear mi with one person and mis with several. The final s is your clue.

Common mix-upTu padre means “your father.” Tú, with an accent, means “you.”

Culture note · Cut-paper decorations

In Mexico, cut-paper banners may hang near a remembrance table with family photos. When you look through snapshots together at home, nuestras fotos means “our photos.”

INTEF · Spanish possessives

Why can one phrase have several meanings?

In su hermana, su can mean his, her, your or their. To name the person, use la hermana de Leo for “Leo’s sister.”
