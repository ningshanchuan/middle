Why does one grow while the other shrinks?

The two hemispheres have opposite seasons. Winter at the North Pole (most ice in March) is summer at the South Pole (least ice in February).

What is the biggest difference between the two poles?

The Arctic is an ocean surrounded by land, with ice floating on the sea. The Antarctic is a continent surrounded by ocean; its sea ice is just the ring around the land.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia Social Studies SS6G / SS7G: locating the 7 continents and 5 oceans is the foundation for 6th-grade regions (Latin America, Canada, Europe, Australia) and 7th-grade regions (Africa, Southwest Asia, Southern & Eastern Asia), and practices Map & Globe Skills (use a legend, compare hemispheres, draw conclusions from maps). Suggestions: (1) play the “World tour” story and have students rank continents by size; (2) use the hemisphere buttons: “Which continents are entirely in the Southern Hemisphere?” (Antarctica, Australia); (3) discuss “four oceans vs five oceans” (the Southern Ocean was officially recognized by National Geographic in 2021); (4) race through the map game as a class competition.
