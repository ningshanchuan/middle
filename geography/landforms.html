Remember them in pairs: land and water “opposites”

Many landforms and bodies of water come in opposite pairs: swap land and water and you get the other one.

Island
↔
Lake

Island: land surrounded by water. Lake: water surrounded by land.

Peninsula
↔
Gulf / bay

Peninsula: land poking into water (water on 3 sides). Gulf: water poking into land (land on 3 sides).

Strait
↔
Isthmus

Strait: narrow water joining two waters. Isthmus: narrow land joining two lands.

Plateau
↔
Plain

Both are flat, but a plateau is high and a plain is low.

⚠️ Common mix-up:A strait is a narrow strip of WATER joining two big bodies of water. An isthmus is a narrow strip of LAND joining two big land areas, like the Isthmus of Panama. The Panama Canal is a human-made waterway, not a natural strait.

Water is soft. How can it cut rock?

The real cutting tools are the sand and stones the river carries. Like sandpaper, they grind the riverbed year after year. This is called erosion. The steeper and faster the river, the faster it cuts.

Why do the canyon walls look like a layer cake?

A plateau is built of rock layers stacked on top of each other, with the oldest at the bottom. As the river cuts down, it slices through them like a knife through cake and shows every layer.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports the Georgia Social Studies standards SS6G and SS7G (e.g., SS6G1, SS6G8, SS6G11, SS7G1, SS7G5, SS7G8: locate major physical features of each region, such as the Andes, the Amazon River, the Iberian Peninsula, the Persian Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz and the Himalayas), plus the map & globe skills of reading contour lines, scale and a compass rose. This page builds the vocabulary first.

Ideas: ① Play the River journey story and ask whether the river is eroding or depositing at each step. ② Raise sea level by 150 m and ask which landforms flood first and why delta cities worry most about rising seas. ③ Turn on Contour lines and find the same plateau in both the 3D model and the topographic map. ④ Have students drag a cross-section through the canyon and describe its shape.
