Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian and Spanish are the six official languages of the United Nations. Interpreters help people join the same discussion in different languages. People speak many other languages, too. Every speaker deserves equal respect.

A language can cross borders, and a place can have many languages. For example, Canada has two official languages, English and French.

A settlement (jùluò, 聚落) is a place where people live together. Around rural settlements you may see fields, pasture or fishing boats. Urban settlements usually have buildings closer together and more shops, industries and services. Water, flat land and good transport routes help settlements grow.

Read a site in four steps: find water, check the terrain, trace the routes, then look for hazards such as flooding.

Worked examples · GDP per person

Annual gross domestic product (GDP) adds up the value of final goods and services produced in an economy during a year. Divide GDP by the population (P) for the same year. The examples use made-up figures.

The North–South idea and cooperation

Geography texts often use “North” and “South” as shorthand for broad patterns of development. Australia lies in the Southern Hemisphere and is commonly grouped with the Global North. Statistical agencies group countries in different ways.

Compare four ways countries work together

Talks between developed and developing countries are often called North–South dialogue. Cooperation among developing countries is called South–South cooperation.

  • Trade exchanges goods and services across borders. For example, a region might export farm products and import machinery.
  • Aid provides funding, supplies or expertise. After a flood, partners might send drinking-water equipment or train local repair crews.
  • The Belt and Road Initiative, proposed by China, includes cooperation on transport infrastructure. For a rail project, compare changes in travel time, construction and upkeep costs, and environmental effects.
  • The UN Sustainable Development Goals give countries shared targets. Goal 6 concerns clean water and sanitation, and Goal 17 focuses on global partnerships.
