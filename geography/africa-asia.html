Water, land, resources & where people live

In all three regions, water and resources are unevenly spread — and that shapes farming, trade and daily life.

💧Water in Africa

Africa

Water is very unevenly spread: the Congo Basin is soaked, while the Sahara and Sahel are parched. Sewage, factory waste and oil spills in the Niger Delta pollute water used for irrigation, industry and drinking.

Tell me more

Many rural people — often women and children — walk hours a day to fetch water.

🌳Poor soil & deforestation

Sub-Saharan Africa

Most rainforest nutrients are stored in the plants; the soil itself is poor. After forest is cut and burned for farms, crops grow for only a few years before heavy rain washes the topsoil away.

Tell me more

So farmers clear more forest — a vicious circle.

🏜️Desertification in the Sahel

Sahel · Africa

Drought plus overgrazing, over-farming and cutting trees for firewood turn Sahel land into desert. Lake Chad has shrunk by about 90% since the 1960s.

What is being done about it?

The “Great Green Wall” project is planting trees across the Sahel. Drag the 💧 slider on the globe to watch Lake Chad.

🛢️Southwest Asia: water vs. oil

Southwest Asia (Middle East)

The lands around the Persian Gulf hold about half of the world’s proven oil, making Saudi Arabia, Iran, Iraq and Kuwait rich exporters, while countries without oil must import it.

Tell me more

Water, though, is scarce. Turkey’s dams on the upper Euphrates and Tigris leave less for Syria and Iraq downstream; Gulf countries turn seawater into drinking water (desalination).

🚱Pollution of the Ganges & Yangtze

India · China

Hundreds of millions of people live along these rivers. Untreated sewage, factory waste and farm chemicals pour in, threatening drinking water and fish.

What is being done about it?

India’s “Clean Ganga” mission and China’s 10-year Yangtze fishing ban (from 2021) aim to bring the rivers back to health.

🌫️Air pollution & flooding in India and China

Southern & Eastern Asia

Coal power, factories, traffic and crop burning bring smog to cities such as New Delhi and Beijing. Both countries are building wind, solar and electric cars.

Tell me more

Summer monsoon rains flood the Ganges–Brahmaputra delta (Bangladesh) and the Yangtze and Huang He basins.
