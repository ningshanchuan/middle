👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports Georgia Social Studies SS6G / SS7G Map & Globe Skills: use longitude to understand how location relates to time, and use maps to explain how geography affects daily life. Suggestions: (1) play the “Why time zones?” story and stress 360° ÷ 24 h = 15°; (2) press “Now” and have students compare Atlanta with Beijing and Tokyo — why is the date different?; (3) drag the Date slider to June and December to see the terminator tilt and polar day/night; (4) discuss why China uses one time zone (Beijing Time, UTC+8) and India a half-hour zone. Note: the game uses standard time, while the world clocks show real time including daylight saving — a good discussion point.
