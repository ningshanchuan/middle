⚠️ Common mix-up:Radio waves and X-rays are both “light” (EM waves) and travel at the same speed in a vacuum, about 300,000 km/s. Only their wavelength, frequency and energy differ. Radio waves are not sound waves!

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports GSE S8P4: develop models and use evidence to explain the properties of electromagnetic waves (reflection, refraction, absorption, transmission), how lenses, mirrors and the eye use them, and the uses of different parts of the EM spectrum.

Try this: in the Reflection scene, have students rotate the mirror and record angle i and angle r, then state the law of reflection themselves. In Refraction, aim the laser straight along the normal (0°) and ask why it doesn’t bend. Prism dispersion is exaggerated for visibility (in real glass, the index for red vs violet differs by only about 0.02).

Common misconceptions to probe: eyes send out light to see; color is “in” the object rather than in the reflected light; convex lenses always magnify; EM waves need a medium to travel.
