Curriculum and unit conventions

The groups follow related topics in the PEP Grade 8–9 textbooks. Some symbols follow Chinese textbook conventions. For example, G means weight, and U means voltage, often written V in US materials. Extension formulas let you explore relationships such as the energy graphs in Georgia S8P2. S8P3 calls for interpreting motion graphs. Calculating acceleration is extension practice.

Examples use 9.8 N/kg for the weight-to-mass ratio unless the problem gives another value. The symbol t often means time. In the heating equation, Δt means temperature change. An interval of 1 K equals an interval of 1 °C.
