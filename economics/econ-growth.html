What Makes Economies Grow

In 1978 the average person in China produced about $157 worth of goods a year — less than a pair of designer sneakers. By 2024 it was about $13,293, more than 80 times as much. What happened?

🏫 Why are schools slow but powerful?

Literacy rises only a little each year, but workers who can read and calculate can run better machines and invent new ideas. Economists call skills and knowledge “human capital.”

🏭 Why do more machines help less and less?

The first machine doubles output; the tenth adds much less — and machines wear out. That’s “diminishing returns.” Machines alone aren’t enough; you also need skills and new ideas.

⛏️ Do natural resources make a country rich?

Oil and minerals bring income for a while, but they run out. Japan has few natural resources yet a high GDP per person, while some resource-rich countries grow slowly.

Mission 4: what did $1 or ¥1 buy?

Inflation is a general rise in prices, so each dollar or yuan buys less. That’s why economists measure growth in “real,” inflation-adjusted terms. Pick a currency and drag the year.
