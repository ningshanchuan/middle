Why can a sunny afternoon turn into a thunderstorm in less than an hour? When a huge body of cold air bumps into a huge body of warm air, the weather changes. ⚠️ Common mix-up:A front is named for the air that is advancing, not just which side is colder. Cold air pushing in and replacing warm air = cold front; warm air advancing over cold air = warm front. The front symbols (triangles or half-circles) point the way the front is moving.

Four air masses that shape North American weather

An air mass is a huge body of air with about the same temperature and humidity throughout, sometimes covering millions of square kilometers. It takes on the character of where it forms: over land it is dry (continental), over ocean it is humid (maritime); near the poles it is cold (polar), near the tropics it is warm (tropical).
