👩‍🏫 Teacher / parent notes

Supports Georgia GSE S7L2: obtain, evaluate and communicate information about how cells, cell structures and cell processes support life (cell theory, plant vs. animal cell structures and functions, organelle analogies, and how the cell membrane controls what enters and leaves: diffusion, osmosis, active transport). This page brings together key topics from the cells unit for independent study and review.

Chinese curriculum link: 人教版 Grade 7 (first semester), Unit 2 “生物体的结构层次”, Chapter 1 “细胞是生命活动的基本单位” (using the microscope, observing plant and animal cells, the life of a cell, the nucleus as the control center). Comparing plant and animal cells and the membrane controlling what enters and leaves are in this chapter; the history of cell theory, membrane transport (passive, active, endocytosis/exocytosis) and plasmolysis are senior-high 必修1《分子与细胞》, used here as enrichment.

Suggested use: watch the 90-second story, rate the topics, start with the 😟 topics and their flashcard decks, then take a timed practice test and follow the score report back to specific lessons. Try Challenge mode once you know the basics. It gives you harder questions and less time. CER answers are self-scored: write a full answer first, then tick the key-point checklist. Common errors: mitochondria are “only in animal cells” (plants have them too); mixing up the cell membrane and the cell wall; thinking a hypertonic solution has more water (it has less); getting the direction of active transport backwards.
