From food chain to food web

A food chain is one straight line of “who eats whom”. Each arrow points to the eater, which is the direction energy flows.

☀️
Energy source

Sun

Gives light energy.

🌾
Level 1

Producer: grass

Stores light energy in sugar by photosynthesis.

🦗
Level 2

Primary consumer: grasshopper

A herbivore that eats plants.

🐸
Level 3

Secondary consumer: frog

A carnivore that eats herbivores.

🐍
Level 4

Tertiary consumer: snake

Eats secondary consumers.

⚠️ Common mix-up:The arrow does not mean “eats”. It means “is eaten by, and energy flows to”. “Grass → grasshopper” means energy flows from the grass into the grasshopper, not that grass eats grasshoppers.
