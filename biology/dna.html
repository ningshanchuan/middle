Where is DNA hiding?

Zoom from your body all the way down to a single DNA “letter”.

🧍

Organism

Your body has about 37 trillion cells.

🔵

Cell

Almost every cell holds a full copy of your DNA.

🟣

Nucleus

DNA is stored inside the nucleus.

🧶

Chromosome

DNA coils tightly into 46 chromosomes (23 pairs).

📏

Gene

A gene is a section of DNA that controls a trait.

🔤

Base pair

A–T and G–C are the “letters” of the code.

⚠️ Common mix-up:A gene is not a separate object. It is just one section of the long DNA strand. One chromosome carries hundreds to thousands of genes.
