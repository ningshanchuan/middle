Plant Structure & Life Cycle

How does water climb to the top of a 300-foot (90 m) redwood tree with no pump? Roots take in water, stems transport, leaves make food and flowers make seeds. Take a flower apart, follow water from root to leaf, then become a bee and pollinate a meadow!

🎯 Challenge: transplant day! Keep the sunlight at 60% or more, but get water loss below 10 mL per hour so the plant won’t wilt. Gardeners really use these tricks!

About 95–99% of the water a plant takes up leaves again by transpiration; only a little is used for photosynthesis and growth. A sunflower can transpire about 1 liter on a sunny day.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Xylem carries water UP; phloem carries sugar both up and down. Remember: xylem = water, phloem = food.

Tropisms: plants that turn

Plants can’t walk, but they can grow toward or away from a stimulus. Move the light or tip the pot over and watch how the stem and root grow.

—stem response
—root response
auxin (growth hormone)

How it works: auxin collects on the shaded side of the stem. Cells there stretch more, so the stem bends toward the light.

💡 Memory hintPhototropism = toward light. Gravitropism = roots down, stems up. Thigmotropism = tendrils wrap around supports when touched.
