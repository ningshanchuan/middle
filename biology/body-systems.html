Human Body Systems: a team effort

What happens inside your body when you sprint to catch the bus? Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs form systems. Peel back the layers of a see-through body, watch blood flow through the heart, follow a bite of food through the digestive tract, and see how the systems team up when you exercise.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports GSE S7L2 (how cells, tissues, organs and organ systems relate, and the functions and interactions of the digestive, respiratory, circulatory, excretory, skeletal, muscular, nervous and immune systems). Ideas: (1) use “Selected system only” to tour all eight systems; (2) play “Blood through the heart”, then ask where the blood color changes and why; (3) in the exercise lab, ask why the digestive system slows down during exercise (blood is sent to the muscles first).
