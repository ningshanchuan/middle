👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Supports GSE S7L3: students construct an explanation of how genetic information is passed on in sexual and asexual reproduction (S7L3a role of genes and chromosomes; S7L3b how cells made by mitosis and meiosis differ). The model uses 2n = 4 (two homologous pairs: red from mom, blue from dad) instead of a human’s 46 so students can follow every chromosome. Suggested flow: scrub the mitosis timeline and have students describe where the chromosomes are in each phase; switch to meiosis and point out crossing over in prophase I (the color swap) and homologous pairs lining up in metaphase I; discuss why every gamete is different (crossing over + independent assortment).

Common misconceptions: DNA is copied twice before meiosis; mitosis changes the chromosome number; sister chromatids are the same thing as homologous chromosomes.
