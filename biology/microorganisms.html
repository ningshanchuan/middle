Helpful microbes

Lactobacillus makes yogurt and pickles, yeast makes bread rise, gut bacteria help digestion and make vitamin K.

🍂

Decomposers

Bacteria and fungi break down dead leaves and animals, returning nutrients to the soil. Without them, Earth would be buried in dead stuff!

🤒

Harmful microbes

Pathogens cause disease: strep throat (bacteria), flu (virus), malaria (protist), athlete’s foot (fungus).

🧼

Staying safe

Wash hands, get vaccinated, cook and refrigerate food. Antibiotics treat bacterial infections; vaccines help the body prevent viral infections.

⚠️ Common mix-up:Colds and flu are caused by viruses, so antibiotics do not help. Overusing antibiotics also helps resistant bacteria spread.
