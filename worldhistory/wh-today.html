Our Connected World: how far has your phone travelled?

The phone in your pocket may contain parts from a dozen countries: cobalt from DR Congo, lithium from Australia, a camera chip from Japan, a screen from South Korea — put together in China or India, then shipped across the Pacific and through the Panama Canal to the Port of Savannah, Georgia. How did the world get this connected? And who sets the rules and tackles global problems?

🏛️ How the UN works

General Assembly & Security Council

General Assembly: all 193 members, one country one vote. Security Council: 15 members, including 5 permanent members — China, France, Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States — each with a veto; it is responsible for international peace and security.

Agencies and peacekeepers

Agencies such as UNICEF, the World Health Organization (WHO), the World Food Programme (WFP) and UNESCO work worldwide. UN peacekeepers wear blue helmets; China sends more peacekeepers than any other permanent Security Council member.

👩‍🏫 Teacher notes

Georgia Standards of Excellence (2016): Grade 6 and 7 economics (SS6E, SS7E) — trade, trade barriers (tariffs, quotas, embargoes), exchange rates; Grade 6 includes the purpose of the European Union and the euro. SS6H3 and the Grade 7 regional history standards give the post-Cold War background; geography standards on location and human activity fit the supply-chain mission. The catalog lists SS6H / SS7H — check numbering against your district’s current version.

China curriculum: unified-edition World History Grade 9 Vol. 2, Unit 6 — the UN and the World Trade Organization (the UN’s purposes and role; the WTO and China’s entry in 2001), the post-Cold War world (a multipolar trend, the EU), modern society (economic globalization, information technology). The China strip matches Chinese History Grade 8 Vol. 2: reform and opening up (the 1978 Third Plenary Session), special economic zones, WTO entry, the Belt and Road Initiative. Grade 9 Morality and Law also covers economic globalization. Lesson numbers vary by printing.
