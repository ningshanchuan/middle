Win the Argument

Want a later school start, a longer recess or a phone rule that makes sense? Yelling louder won’t get it. A claim, strong evidence and clear reasoning, plus an honest answer to the other side, just might.

Your mission: dissect a sample argument, test evidence like a detective, then build a case on three real questions and earn a perfect 16 / 16 from the judge.

🧩 Why do teachers say “CER”?

CER = Claim + Evidence + Reasoning. Chinese essays use almost the same three parts: 论点, 论据, 论证. American teachers also want a counterclaim and a rebuttal: admit what the other side thinks, then show why your claim still stands.

    🚩 Five fallacies to catch

    Bandwagon

    “Everybody does it, so it must be right.” Popular is not the same as true.

    Ad hominem

    Attacking the person instead of the idea. Latin for “to the person”.

    Either-or (false choice)

    Pretending there are only two options when there are many in between.

    Slippery slope

    “One small step will slide all the way to disaster,” with no proof for each step.

    Hasty generalization

    A big conclusion from one or two cases: “My grandpa never exercised and lived to 95, so exercise is useless.”
