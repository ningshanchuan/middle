Compare with water vapor. Did the molecules change?
Balanced equation 2H₂O(l) → 2H₂(g) + O₂(g) (electricity) Mainland textbook notation 2H₂O ═ 2H₂↑ + O₂↑（通电）

Water contains the elements hydrogen and oxygen. During electrolysis the atoms rearrange into hydrogen and oxygen gases. Count four hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms on either side of the equation. Two hydrogen molecules form for every oxygen molecule, giving an ideal volume ratio of 2 to 1 at the same temperature and pressure.

Worked examples and a challenge

Example 1. You collect 4 mL of oxygen. At the same temperature and pressure, what volume of hydrogen does the ideal ratio predict?

Set the slider until the positive tube reads 4 mL. Then read the negative tube. The hydrogen volume is twice the oxygen volume. 2 × 4 mL = 8 mL

Example 2 Given 15 mL of gas in total at the same temperature and pressure and an ideal volume ratio of 2 to 1.

Find the volume of each gas.

Solve by dividing the total into three parts. Oxygen gets one part. 15 mL ÷ 3 = 5 mL Hydrogen gets two. 2 × 5 mL = 10 mL

Oxygen is 5 mL and hydrogen is 10 mL. Check your answer against both readings in the diagram.

Challenge · In the ideal model, what capacity is needed for the hydrogen tube at 9 mL of oxygen?

At least 18 mL. Treat 9 mL as one part, then find two parts. This volume goes beyond the scale in the model. 2 × 9 mL = 18 mL

Why might the collected ratio exceed 2 to 1?

At room temperature and equal gas partial pressures, oxygen is more soluble in water than hydrogen. If a larger fraction of the oxygen dissolves, the measured ratio rises. Leaks can also affect the readings.

Making water from gases · History and a real apparatus

In 1783, Antoine Lavoisier reported making water from hydrogen and oxygen and interpreted the result as evidence that water was a compound. In 1784, Henry Cavendish published his earlier experiments in which burning hydrogen produced water. Making water from the gases and splitting water into gases give evidence from opposite directions.

Classify matter by its particles

O oxygen H hydrogen Na⁺ sodium ion Cl⁻ chloride ion

  • A pure substance is one substance. A mixture contains two or more substances.
  • An elemental substance is a pure substance made of one element. A compound contains two or more elements chemically combined in a fixed ratio.
  • In this textbook classification, a compound of two elements, including oxygen, is called an oxide. Water fits this definition.
Element and elemental substance An element can mean a kind of atom or the pure substance made from it. Oxygen in a water molecule is an atom bonded to hydrogen. Oxygen gas is a substance made of oxygen molecules.

Water on Earth and in China

    Save water and protect its sources

    China’s Ministry of Water Resources estimated 2,578.25 billion cubic meters of water resources in 2023, 6.6% below the long-term average. Water resources are generally more abundant in the south than in the north. The same place can also have wetter and drier seasons.

    • At home, turn off the tap while brushing and repair leaks.
    • Treat sewage and industrial wastewater so it meets discharge standards. Keep motor oil out of storm drains.
    • Rain can carry fertilizer and pesticides from farms to streams. Careful application and vegetated strips along streams can reduce runoff pollution.
