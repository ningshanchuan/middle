Common signed valences

For a single-atom ion or a whole polyatomic ion, the signed value equals the ion’s charge. Write the charge at the upper right, sign last, as in Ca²⁺. In an elemental substance such as O₂, the oxidation number is zero. The table shows the values in the listed examples.

Elements
Polyatomic ions
RememberIn a compound’s formula, keep a polyatomic ion together. Write one hydroxide as OH and two as (OH)₂. The outer 2 counts two O atoms and two H atoms.

An ionic formula such as CaO gives the simplest ratio of its ions. A molecular formula such as H₂O₂ gives the actual number of atoms of each element in a molecule.

Worked example: calcium in Ca(OH)₂

Given Ca(OH)₂ and the unitless relative atomic masses Ca = 40, O = 16 and H = 1. Find the relative formula mass and the mass percent of calcium. Each hydroxide contains one O and one H. The 2 outside parentheses counts two hydroxides, so the formula gives 2 O, 2 H and 1 Ca.
The relative formula mass is 74 (unitless). Calcium makes up about 54.05% by mass.
An element with more atoms may contribute less mass. Use both the atom count and the relative atomic mass. The Chinese curriculum introduces this calculation under 相对分子质量. In English, use relative molecular mass for molecules and relative formula mass for ionic compounds. Both are unitless.
Relative formula mass and formula mass

With the classroom masses, the relative formula mass of Ca(OH)₂ is 74. The formula mass per formula unit is about 74 u (unified atomic mass units).

Try each problem, then reveal the steps

How many O atoms are represented by 2Al₂(SO₄)₃? What percent of aluminum sulfate’s mass is oxygen?

Use 27 for Al, 32 for S and 16 for O as the relative atomic masses.

Each formula unit has 3 sulfate groups, each with 4 O atoms. Two formula units represent 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 O atoms.
One formula unit gives the mass percent. Using two would double both masses in the ratio and give the same result.
Find the signed valence of Fe in FeCl₃ and name the compound in both languages.
In FeCl₃, the signed valence of Cl is −1. Call the value for Fe x. Multiply each value by its atom count and set the sum equal to zero.
English: iron(III) chloride. Chinese: 氯化铁.
