When does a reaction happen?

In double displacement (also called double replacement), two compounds swap parts to make two new compounds. A product may then break down. For these solutions, look for a precipitate, a gas, or water. If mixing leaves the same ions dissolved, there is no net reaction.

Worked example · Calcium chloride and sodium carbonate
  1. Swap partners. Calcium and carbonate give calcium carbonate. Sodium and chloride give sodium chloride.
  2. Check the table. Calcium carbonate forms a white precipitate. Sodium and chloride ions remain dissolved.
  3. Balance. Put a coefficient of 2 before sodium chloride so each side has two sodium atoms and two chlorine atoms.
CaCl₂(aq) + Na₂CO₃(aq) → CaCO₃(s) + 2 NaCl(aq)

Chinese textbooks often mark a new precipitate with ↓ and a new gas with ↑ in cases like these. US textbooks commonly use (s) for solid, (g) for gas, (l) for liquid, and (aq) for dissolved in water.

Worked calculation · Salt yield

Given. You start with 5.00 g of crude salt and recover 3.60 g of dry crystals.

Find. What percentage of the crude sample’s mass did you recover?

Calculate. Divide the dry product mass by the mass of crude salt used.

(3.60 g ÷ 5.00 g) × 100% = 72.0%

Answer. The yield is 72.0%. Uncheck “Wet crystals” and set the loss slider to 0.60 g to reproduce the result.

Practice · Wet and dry crystals

A 6.00 g crude salt sample gives crystals weighing 4.80 g before drying and 4.20 g after drying. What is the dry yield? By how many percentage points did the apparent yield fall?

Show steps

Divide each product mass by 6.00 g, then subtract the two percentages.

(4.20 g ÷ 6.00 g) × 100% = 70.0%
(4.80 g ÷ 6.00 g) × 100% = 80.0%

The dry yield is 70.0%, a drop of 10.0 percentage points.

    N, P, K, and compound fertilizers

    Nitrogen supports leaf and stem growth. Phosphorus helps transfer energy and supports root development. Potassium helps regulate water in plants. In the Chinese textbook, a compound fertilizer supplies at least two of these nutrients. Potassium nitrate supplies nitrogen and potassium.

    Appearance and solubility offer clues. Ammonia from the base test indicates ammonium. Nitrate fertilizers also supply nitrogen and give no ammonia in this test.

    Ground phosphate rock contains phosphorus. How much plants can take up depends on the soil and the rock.

    Three numbers on a US fertilizer bag 课外拓展 · Enrichment

    A 10–5–8 grade reports 10% total nitrogen, 5% available phosphate as P₂O₅ equivalent, and 8% soluble potash as K₂O equivalent, all by mass.

    UGA Cooperative Extension · Fertilizer labels

    How much fertilizer, and when?

    Use soil test results and crop needs to choose the amount, timing, and placement. Wait to apply fertilizer if heavy rain is forecast. Runoff can carry excess nitrogen and phosphorus into waterways, where algal blooms can lead to low oxygen.
